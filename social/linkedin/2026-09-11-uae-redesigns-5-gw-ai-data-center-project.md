AI infrastructure is becoming a physical-resilience issue, not only a compute-procurement issue.

Reuters reports that the UAE is revising plans for a 5 GW AI data-center project after the Iran war exposed the vulnerability of concentrating critical infrastructure in one location. Instead of a single 10-square-mile Abu Dhabi campus, planners are considering a distributed network across the country.

The options reportedly under review include:
- geographic distribution to reduce concentration risk;
- air defenses around strategic facilities;
- placing some infrastructure underground.

For banks and other regulated firms, resilience reviews of AI platforms now need to include geopolitical exposure, power and network dependencies, physical security, and credible multi-region failover—not just cloud SLAs.
