Vendor policy changes are becoming a material part of AI governance.

Anthropic has updated its Usage Policy, effective November 12. The revision clarifies restrictions around deceptive activity, influence operations, surveillance and weapons-related misuse, and adds controls for systems that autonomously take physical actions.

For regulated firms, the most relevant change is the clarification of requirements for high-risk health and finance use cases. This should trigger three actions:
- map affected use cases to the revised policy;
- confirm how technical and human controls evidence compliance;
- update vendor-risk, exception and change-management processes.

AI governance cannot stop at model approval. Provider terms, model capabilities and deployed workflows all change—and controls need to remain aligned with all three.
