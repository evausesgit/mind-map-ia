AI-enabled cyberattacks against financial institutions are no longer a hypothetical risk.

India's cybersecurity agency has identified the group and tools behind a wave of AI-driven attacks on financial firms, with at least two tools linked to China, according to Reuters.

For banks, this should accelerate three controls:
- detection based on behaviour, not only known signatures;
- red-team exercises that include autonomous and adaptive attack chains;
- tighter governance of internal agents, credentials and tool permissions.

The same capabilities that improve defensive speed can also compress an attacker's reconnaissance and exploitation cycle.
