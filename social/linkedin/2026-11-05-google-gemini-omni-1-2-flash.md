Enterprise video generation is becoming cheaper, longer and easier to iterate.

Google has made Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash generally available through its API and business products. It supports native 15-second clips and multi-turn editing, while pricing falls 30% to $0.07 per second for 720p generation; enterprise deployment options include provisioned throughput and SynthID watermarking.

The interesting shift is workflow economics: teams can edit an existing generation instead of repeatedly starting over. For regulated firms, however, provenance, brand consistency, approval controls and retention policies need to mature alongside production capability.

- Compare cost per approved asset, not cost per generation.
- Preserve prompts, references and edit history for auditability.
- Define when watermarking is sufficient and when disclosure is required.
