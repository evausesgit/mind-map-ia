AI-enabled cyber operations are moving beyond advice toward execution and orchestration.

Anthropic’s September threat report describes malicious Claude use disrupted between December 2025 and August 2026. Across the cases, the company observed AI supporting or directly executing reconnaissance, phishing, exploitation, persistence, and data exfiltration.

One suspected Russian espionage actor reportedly targeted more than 20 organizations and used AI workflows to rebuild malware when security products detected it. The practical warning is clear:

- static signatures impose less cost when attackers can iterate automatically;
- identity, cloud access, and behavioral telemetry become even more important;
- agent deployments need strong permissions, monitoring, and rapid containment.

For regulated enterprises, AI security governance must cover both defensive use and the accelerating capabilities available to attackers.
