AI financing is becoming a financial-stability topic, not only a technology-sector story.

The Bank of England says hyperscaler bond issuance is set to exceed $305 billion in 2026, around 8% of global investment-grade issuance. It also highlights growing links across equities, public debt, private credit and more opaque financing structures.

- AI concentration can transmit stress across asset classes.
- Financing structure deserves the same scrutiny as model capability.
- Banks need exposure mapping that reaches beyond direct technology lending.

For AI and risk leaders, this is a reminder to connect technology strategy with counterparty, market and concentration-risk governance.
