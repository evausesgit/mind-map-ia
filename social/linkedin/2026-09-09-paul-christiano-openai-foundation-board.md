Frontier-model governance is increasingly becoming a board-level design question.

OpenAI has appointed Paul Christiano, founder of the Alignment Research Center and a former OpenAI alignment lead, to its Foundation Board and Safety and Security Committee. He will also serve as a non-voting observer on the OpenAI Group PBC board.

The governance signal matters:
- technical safety expertise is being placed closer to formal decisions;
- independent challenge is explicitly framed as a board responsibility;
- oversight must connect model evaluations to deployment and escalation thresholds.

For enterprise boards and risk committees, the parallel is clear: AI oversight needs members who can interrogate technical evidence, not only review policy summaries.
