Enterprise AI is moving from assistants that answer questions to agents that can complete work across systems.

Google Cloud's new Gemini agent is positioned as a universal work interface: it can use organisational context, plan a task, call tools, connect to business systems and return a finished deliverable. Google also highlights model routing, cost controls and enterprise administration.

For AI leaders, the architecture is only half the story. The operating model now matters just as much:

- Which actions require human approval?
- How are identity, entitlements and evidence logged across tools?
- Can cost and quality be measured per workflow, not just per model call?

The strongest enterprise deployments will treat agents as governed digital operators, not smarter chatbots.
