The next bottleneck for enterprise agents may be business definitions, not model intelligence.

Snowflake is putting governed context and central policy controls around agents through Business Ontology and Cortex AI Gateway. The design targets a familiar problem: systems can define the same metric differently, while agents need one approved meaning and an auditable route to action.

- Assign owners to business concepts and calculations
- Control model, tool and MCP access centrally
- Capture every agent action for audit and incident review

For AI leaders, the practical question is how this changes architecture, governance, resilience and cost decisions in production.
