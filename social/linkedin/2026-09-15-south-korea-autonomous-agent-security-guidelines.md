South Korea is translating agentic-AI risk into a practical control model.

KISA is preparing version 2.0 of its AI Security Guide for autonomous agents operating across software, networks and physical systems. The draft approach separates duties among developers, service providers and enterprise users.

Controls under consideration include:
- constrained access to tools and tamper-resistant decision logs;
- real-time shutdown and incident tracking;
- human approval for high-risk financial, operational or physical actions.

The guide is not final, but the direction is useful now. Enterprises can inventory agents, enforce least privilege and define approval thresholds before regulation makes those controls mandatory.
