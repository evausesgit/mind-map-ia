Open-weight models are moving deeper into cybersecurity and software assurance.

Z.ai's GLM-5.3 is being positioned as a strong open-weight model for vulnerability detection and security-oriented coding tasks, with reported API pricing around $1.40 / $4.40 per million tokens.

For enterprise AI leaders, the signal is not just model performance. It is cost compression in a domain where capability can be defensive or offensive depending on controls.

Three practical questions matter:
- Which security workflows can safely benefit from lower-cost model inference?
- How do we validate model provenance, license, and supply-chain risk?
- What monitoring is needed when code/security agents act at scale?

The open-model ecosystem is becoming more strategically important — and more governance-sensitive.
