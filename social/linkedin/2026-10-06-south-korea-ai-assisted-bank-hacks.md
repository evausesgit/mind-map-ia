AI-enabled cyber risk is moving from scenario planning into banking investigations.

South Korean President Lee Jae Myung says AI models are believed to have been used in recent attacks against commercial banks. Police have opened a full-scale investigation, while the government is calling for cyber defences designed for the AI era.

The allegation still needs technical attribution. But financial institutions should not wait for perfect attribution before adapting controls:
- test detection against AI-assisted reconnaissance and attack automation;
- shorten the path from anomaly detection to containment;
- connect AI governance, cyber intelligence and third-party risk teams.

The strategic shift is clear: AI changes not only the volume of attacks, but potentially their speed, adaptability and cost structure.
