AI agents are crossing a line that matters: from providing information to taking regulated financial actions.

The UK FCA now says that agents initiating transactions, moving money, arranging borrowing or making investment decisions may require authorisation. It also makes clear that regulated firms and senior managers remain accountable when third-party AI is involved.

For financial institutions, three design requirements stand out:
- identify the agent, its authority and the customer’s consent;
- control autonomy in real time and under stress;
- define liability, escalation and redress before deployment.

Agentic AI governance is becoming part of the regulated operating model, not a separate innovation exercise.
