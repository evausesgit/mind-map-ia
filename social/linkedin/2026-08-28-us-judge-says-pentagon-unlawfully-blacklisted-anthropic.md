AI procurement is becoming a governance issue, not just a vendor-selection exercise.

A US federal judge ruled that the Department of Defense acted unlawfully when it designated Anthropic as a supply-chain risk after the company refused certain military uses of its models, including surveillance or autonomous weapons.

For enterprise AI teams, the case is a reminder that acceptable-use policies, public-sector contracts, and model-risk governance can collide quickly.

Key takeaways:
- AI vendors are increasingly setting boundaries on high-risk use cases.
- Buyers need contingency plans when policy, regulation, and procurement diverge.
- Governance records matter when decisions are challenged.
