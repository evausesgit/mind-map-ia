A useful open-weight release is not only about benchmark position. It is also about what can run locally, under a licence enterprises can understand.

Thinking Machines has released Inkling 2 Mini, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 26B total parameters and 5.4B active parameters under Apache 2.0. Its looped architecture is designed to spend more computation per token, while remaining small enough for laptop-class deployment.

For enterprise AI teams, the practical questions are clear:
- Can local inference improve data control and resilience?
- Does the model card provide enough evidence for risk review?
- How does total cost compare with a managed API at production load?

Open weights expand deployment choice, but governance still depends on evaluation, patching, provenance and operational controls.
