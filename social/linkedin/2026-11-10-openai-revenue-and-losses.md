Rapid AI revenue growth does not yet mean mature AI economics.

OpenAI's third-quarter revenue reportedly reached $10 billion, up nearly 50%, while its quarterly loss widened to around $10.9 billion, according to the Wall Street Journal as reported by Reuters.

The numbers highlight the central tension in frontier AI:
- demand can scale extremely quickly;
- compute, talent and infrastructure commitments can scale just as fast;
- enterprise pricing may remain strategically subsidised while platforms compete for share.

AI leaders should therefore assess vendor sustainability, concentration and exit options alongside capability and unit price.
