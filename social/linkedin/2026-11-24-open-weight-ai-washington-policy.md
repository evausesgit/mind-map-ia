Open-weight AI is moving from a technical choice to a geopolitical policy decision.

Reuters reports that the debate has returned to the centre of Washington policy, linking Chinese model adoption, advanced-chip access and US industrial strategy.

- Open weights can reduce vendor dependency.
- Provenance and jurisdiction now affect model-risk decisions.
- Future controls may shape which models enterprises can deploy.

AI leaders should maintain a model inventory that captures licence, origin, hosting, security evaluation and substitution options, not just benchmark scores.
