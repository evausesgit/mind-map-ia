Enterprise AI quality often depends less on the model alone than on whether the system can find, inspect, and verify the right internal evidence.

Mistral introduced Agentic Search, a retrieval layer designed for complex enterprise documents. The company says it improves results while reducing turns, token use, and latency on FinanceBench and OfficeQA Pro, and makes the capability available through Mistral Search Toolkit and Libraries in Studio and Vibe.

Why this matters:
- retrieval is becoming an agentic workflow, not a single vector-search call;
- finance and office-document benchmarks are directly relevant to enterprise deployments;
- portable tooling may help teams work within cloud or on-prem isolation boundaries.

For builders, the practical question is how to benchmark retrieval end-to-end: answer accuracy, citation quality, latency, cost, and auditability.
