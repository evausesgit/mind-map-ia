Central banks are bringing AI risk into the same conversation as digital money and operational resilience.

The Bank of Japan has called for safeguards against cyber and operational risks amplified by AI. The signal matters because payment systems and digital-money infrastructure combine high availability requirements with complex third-party dependencies.

- Model governance must connect to cyber controls.
- Resilience plans should assume AI-enabled attacks and failures.
- Vendor and data dependencies need central-bank-grade visibility.

For financial institutions, AI controls cannot remain isolated inside innovation teams. They belong in the operational-resilience framework.
