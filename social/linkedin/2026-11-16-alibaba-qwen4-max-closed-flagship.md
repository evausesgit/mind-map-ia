The latest Qwen release makes an important distinction: model capability and model openness are separate strategic choices.

Alibaba's Qwen4-Max is a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model, activating 71 billion parameters per token and supporting a one-million-token context window. The flagship is available through Alibaba's services and API, but its weights are not open.

What stands out for enterprise AI teams:
- Alibaba says smaller Qwen4 models will receive open weights, but has not set a date.
- The closed flagship/open smaller-model split creates different options for maximum capability versus deployment control.
- Vendor benchmarks and cost claims still need independent validation on real workloads.

For AI leaders, the practical question is not simply whether a model is “open.” It is which layer of the model family is open, under what licence, and whether the deployment economics and governance controls match the use case.
