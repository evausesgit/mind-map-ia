The US frontier-AI debate is moving from voluntary commitments toward a possible legal duty of care.

Reuters reports that Senate negotiators are discussing rules requiring AI developers to design products to prevent catastrophic risks, including AI-enabled nuclear or biological weapons. The proposal could also give the federal government power to block releases deemed unsafe, with developers able to challenge decisions in federal court.

Three design questions will matter:
- how “catastrophic risk” is defined and evidenced;
- what tests and documentation support a release decision;
- whether federal rules pre-empt stronger state requirements.

The text is still under negotiation. Even so, regulated enterprises should prepare for more structured evidence from model vendors: evaluation results, incident reporting, deployment constraints, and change-management records.
