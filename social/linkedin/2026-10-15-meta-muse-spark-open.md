Open-weight AI is moving beyond compact local models.

Meta’s Muse Spark Open is a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with roughly 816B total parameters, 42B active per token, and a context window of up to one million tokens. It is designed for coding and long-running agentic work across text, images, audio, and video.

Three practical takeaways:
- open weights can improve control, inspection, and deployment flexibility;
- “open” does not mean lightweight: the BF16 checkpoint is about 1.5 TB;
- infrastructure, licensing, security testing, and independent evaluation remain decisive for enterprise adoption.

For AI leaders, the relevant question is not whether open-weight models can compete, but which workloads justify the operational burden in exchange for greater control and reduced vendor dependency.
