Open-weight AI is becoming part of the cyber-defence stack—and of technology strategy.

A new CSIS analysis estimates that open-weight models are only four to six months behind advanced closed frontier systems. It highlights Hugging Face’s use of Z.AI’s GLM-5.2 during a recent intrusion investigation as an example of the value of models that defenders can inspect, adapt and run locally.

The trade-off is real:
- local deployment can improve sovereignty and incident response;
- inspectability supports specialised defensive tuning;
- unrestricted redistribution can also expand misuse.

For regulated enterprises, “open versus closed” is not a single risk ranking. It calls for use-case-specific evaluation across capability, provenance, licence, deployment boundary, monitoring and patch governance.
