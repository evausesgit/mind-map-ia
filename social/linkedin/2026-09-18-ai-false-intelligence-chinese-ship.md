An AI hallucination reportedly came close to becoming a geopolitical event.

CNN reports that a US military analyst used a chatbot that misidentified cargo aboard a Chinese ship. The resulting intelligence report was treated as credible enough for aircraft to be airborne and personnel to prepare an interception before deeper verification found the assessment was false.

For high-consequence AI workflows, “human in the loop” is not sufficient unless the human has time, evidence and authority to challenge the output. Organisations need:
- mandatory source provenance and confidence labels;
- independent verification before action;
- escalation thresholds tied to impact, not only model scores.

The lesson for financial institutions is direct: polished AI output can inherit institutional authority unless controls explicitly prevent it.
