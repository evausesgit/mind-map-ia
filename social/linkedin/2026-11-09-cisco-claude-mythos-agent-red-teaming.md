Agent security needs testing that can reason, adapt and still stay inside strict boundaries.

Cisco is adding Anthropic’s Claude Mythos to AI Defense for adaptive red teaming. Tests are restricted to registered, customer-authorized targets, attributed to a named operator and recorded for audit.

The design highlights three controls worth carrying into enterprise programs:
- explicit authorization and scope;
- human-defined objectives and containment;
- complete traceability of actions.

As agents gain tool access, static prompt tests are not enough. Financial institutions need end-to-end adversarial testing of connectors, identities, permissions and business actions.
