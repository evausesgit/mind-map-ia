AI-assisted AI research may be creating a measurable feedback loop.

METR estimates that Anthropic's benchmark progress since October 2025 has been 1.9 times its earlier rate, with a wide 95% confidence interval of 1.5 to 2.8 times. The independent evaluator tentatively attributes perhaps 80% of the current acceleration to AI-assisted R&D—but explicitly stresses uncertainty and methodological limits.

This is not a clean forecast of future capability. It is a governance signal:
- evaluation cycles may need to shorten;
- model roadmaps can become obsolete faster;
- third-party evidence and uncertainty ranges matter more than headline scores.

AI leaders should treat capability monitoring as a continuous control, not an annual review.
