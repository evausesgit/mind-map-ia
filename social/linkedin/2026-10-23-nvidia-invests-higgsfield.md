Nvidia’s investment strategy continues to extend well beyond chips.

The company joined TCV in an extension of AI-video startup Higgsfield’s Series B. The move reinforces a familiar flywheel: fund application-layer companies, accelerate new workloads and expand demand for the underlying compute platform.

For enterprise AI leaders, three questions matter more than the headline:
- where value accrues between infrastructure, models and applications;
- whether inference economics support durable margins;
- how dependency on one accelerated-computing ecosystem affects resilience.

The AI investment cycle is increasingly an ecosystem strategy, not a collection of isolated bets.
