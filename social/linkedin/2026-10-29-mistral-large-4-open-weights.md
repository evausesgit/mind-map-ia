Mistral has made Large 4 available as open weights — a meaningful European entry in the frontier-model race.

The model uses a mixture-of-experts architecture with one trillion total parameters and 52 billion active parameters. Mistral is positioning it for coding, agentic workflows and sensitive enterprise domains such as cybersecurity, finance and law.

For enterprise AI leaders, the strategic value is control: teams can evaluate and potentially host the model in their own environment. But “open weights” is not the same as low operational complexity.

- Validate the licence and intended use.
- Benchmark on internal workloads, not vendor claims alone.
- Include infrastructure, security and model-lifecycle costs in the business case.

Europe now has a stronger sovereign-model option. The next test is whether it delivers competitive quality and economics in production.
