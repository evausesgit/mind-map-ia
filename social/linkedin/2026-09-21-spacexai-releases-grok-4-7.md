Frontier-model competition is increasingly about sustained work, deployment economics, and measurable safeguards—not only headline reasoning scores.

SpaceXAI has released Grok 4.7 for coding, agentic tasks, and knowledge work. The model offers a 500,000-token context window and starts at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens.

Three figures worth watching from the vendor's own evaluation:
- 46.3% on CursorBench 4.0, versus 40.4% for Grok 4.6;
- 38.0% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, versus 20.3%;
- 3.3% of risky dual-use prompts allowed through HackerBench v0.3.

For enterprise buyers, the practical question is whether these gains survive independent testing on real workflows. Price-performance, long-running reliability, regional processing, and security behavior should all be evaluated together before adoption.
