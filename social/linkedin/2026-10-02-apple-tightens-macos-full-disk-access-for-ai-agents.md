Desktop AI agents are forcing operating systems to revisit permissions designed for a less autonomous software era.

Apple says future macOS controls will require “very explicit” user action before an app receives Full Disk Access — a permission that can expose files, mail, messages and browsing history.

For enterprise AI leaders, the lesson is broader than macOS:
- model safety cannot substitute for least-privilege access;
- agent identity and entitlements need continuous enforcement;
- user consent must be specific, visible and revocable.

Agent governance has to extend to the execution layer, not stop at the model endpoint.
