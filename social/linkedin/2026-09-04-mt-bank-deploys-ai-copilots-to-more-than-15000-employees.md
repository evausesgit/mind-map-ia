Banking AI adoption is shifting from pilots to broad internal deployment.

AI News reports that M&T Bank has deployed AI copilots to more than 15,000 employees after a multi-year technology overhaul. The reported use cases include customer service, software development, drafting reports and risk-management workflows.

The interesting lesson is organizational, not just technical. A deployment at this scale requires data access controls, change management, policy, training, usage monitoring and a clear split between low-risk productivity tasks and regulated decision-support workflows.

For AI leaders in financial services, this is a useful benchmark:

- enterprise readiness matters as much as model capability;
- internal copilots need measurable productivity and risk controls;
- risk, compliance and technology teams must operate from the same playbook.
