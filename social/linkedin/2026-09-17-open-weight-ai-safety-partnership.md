Open-weight AI needs safety controls that remain useful after deployment—not only safeguards applied at release. Baseten’s Base Labs, Hugging Face and Goodfire are partnering on transparent evaluation and monitoring infrastructure for open models.

The immediate concern is “abliteration,” a family of techniques used to remove model refusals. Hugging Face currently lists more than 6,000 models described as abliterated.

The partnership’s technical design is still to be published, but the direction is important:

- Treat the exact weights and fine-tune as governed artifacts.
- Re-test safeguards after every adaptation or quantization.
- Monitor runtime behavior rather than trusting the base model card.

Open weights can improve inspectability and control, but only when deployment discipline matches that openness.
