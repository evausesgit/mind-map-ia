OpenAI is now publicly calling for mandatory, capability-based US safety regulation for frontier AI.

Its proposal covers common testing, independent assessments, cybersecurity, incident reporting, and shared thresholds for slowing or stopping development. OpenAI also endorsed four California bills addressing independent assessors, auditor standards, youth safety, and safeguards against AI-enabled biological threats.

For regulated enterprises, three signals stand out:
- voluntary lab commitments may become auditable obligations;
- serious AI incidents are moving toward formal reporting regimes;
- requirements should follow capability and risk rather than apply uniformly to every model.

The important next question is how these principles translate into enforceable standards, evidence requirements, and international interoperability.
