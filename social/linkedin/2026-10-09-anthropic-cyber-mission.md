AI cybersecurity is moving from demos into operational defence.

Anthropic has launched a Cyber Mission with two initial tracks: critical infrastructure and open-source software. The Critical Infrastructure Defense Program starts with 11 security, consulting and industrial partners; OSS Scanner offers enrolled open-source projects free recurring scans from Anthropic's strongest models.

Three details matter for enterprise leaders:
- vulnerability discovery is being connected to triage and remediation workflows;
- the scanner's reports are model-generated and not human-reviewed;
- Anthropic expects more than 90% true positives, so validation and maintainer capacity remain essential.

The strategic question is no longer whether models can find flaws. It is whether organisations can verify, prioritise and patch faster than attackers can exploit them—without flooding already constrained security teams with low-quality findings.
