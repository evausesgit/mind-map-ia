Open weights are expanding enterprise choice—but they are not removing the infrastructure trade-off.

Reflection’s Beam is a 501B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 23B parameters active per token, a one-million-token context window and an Apache 2.0 licence. It targets coding and agentic workloads, with support for Transformers, vLLM and SGLang.

The practical reality:
- BF16, FP8 and NVFP4 checkpoints are available.
- Even the roughly 280 GB NVFP4 checkpoint requires several high-end GPUs for realistic serving.
- Vendor-reported benchmarks still need workload-specific, independent validation.

For AI leaders, the decision is not “open versus closed” in isolation. It is quality, control, security, observability and total cost per useful task—measured on your own workloads.
