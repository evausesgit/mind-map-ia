Document AI is becoming smaller, more deployable and more open.

LightOnOCR-3 comes in 0.8B, 1B and 4B parameter variants and combines transcription with layout detection, image description and extraction from charts and scientific figures. The Apache 2.0 release creates practical options for local evaluation and controlled deployment.

For regulated enterprises, that matters because document workflows often contain sensitive or confidential data. Open-weight deployment can improve data control and reduce API dependency—but only after testing:
- accuracy on real internal layouts and languages;
- resistance to malformed or adversarial documents;
- traceability from extracted fields back to source pages.

The right benchmark is not a public leaderboard. It is error cost on your own documents.
