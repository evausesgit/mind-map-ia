The capital requirements of frontier AI continue to reset private-market scale.

OpenAI has reportedly discussed a new raise with large investors at a valuation of about $1.2 trillion. Its March round closed with $122 billion in committed capital at an $852 billion valuation, while CEO Sam Altman says an IPO will not happen in 2026.

For enterprise buyers and financial institutions, this is more than a valuation story:
- compute and infrastructure commitments are becoming strategic financing questions;
- concentration around a few model providers is increasing;
- vendor resilience must be assessed alongside performance and price.

Exceptional capital access can accelerate capability, but it also raises the execution bar implied by the valuation.
