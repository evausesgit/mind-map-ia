China is translating frontier-AI risk into concrete agent controls.

Its safety frameworks explicitly consider systems acquiring resources, replicating themselves or seeking power. More immediately, May guidelines require developers to detect, intervene in, block and recover from improper agent behaviour while keeping final decision authority with users.

The practical controls are familiar but important:
- behavioural monitoring and intervention;
- recovery mechanisms for autonomous actions;
- explicit human authority at decision points.

For global firms, this adds another governance regime to map against EU, UK and US requirements. The terminology differs, but the operating model converges on traceability, containment, human override and tested recovery.
