The AI investment debate is moving from capability to cost of capital.

Meta and other AI-linked shares sold off after investors focused on the financing required for continued data-centre expansion. Meta’s credit-risk indicator also reached a record high, showing that concern is no longer confined to equity multiples.

For AI leaders, the message is practical:
- connect infrastructure commitments to measurable adoption and unit economics;
- preserve flexibility across models, clouds and deployment patterns;
- make utilisation, latency, token cost and business outcomes visible together.

The next phase of enterprise AI will be judged as much by capital discipline as by model quality.
