The AI infrastructure contest is increasingly about software efficiency, not only chip specifications.

Amazon is acquiring Decart’s optimization stack and research team in a transaction reported at $7.5 billion. Decart’s software can optimize workloads across Nvidia, Amazon and Google hardware, while AWS prepares to sell Trainium chips directly to customers.

For enterprise AI leaders, this matters in three ways:
- better optimization can reduce inference cost and weaken hardware lock-in;
- portability claims must be tested on real workloads, not vendor benchmarks;
- procurement should compare cost per completed task, migration effort and operational resilience.

The strategic advantage may go to the platform that makes alternative accelerators easiest to adopt—not simply the one with the fastest silicon.
