Microsoft’s Maia 300 announcement is not just another chip launch. It is a cloud operating-model decision.

Microsoft plans production deployment from 2027 and says Maia will become its primary inference architecture across products and services. Reuters reports potential deployment at more than one million chips next year.

The enterprise implications are clear:
- hyperscalers are internalising more of the AI stack;
- inference economics are becoming a strategic differentiator;
- customers must assess both lower cost and deeper platform dependency.

For banks and regulated firms, the right question is not simply whether custom silicon is faster. It is whether performance, resilience, portability and contractual transparency improve together.
