Agentic AI risk is moving from abstract threat models into incident response.

Chainalysis says an attacker used what it calls “offensive agentic techniques” to access account identity data, including names, work emails and organisational roles. It reports no evidence so far that investigation content or authentication secrets were accessed, and the investigation remains ongoing.

Three enterprise takeaways:
- agent permissions and tool access must be treated as privileged access;
- identity metadata can still enable convincing follow-on phishing;
- incident disclosures should separate confirmed facts from assumptions about agent autonomy.

The key question is not whether an AI acted alone. It is whether controls can detect and stop machine-speed attack sequences.
