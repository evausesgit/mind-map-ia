Open-weight AI competition is no longer a two-way story between US proprietary labs and Chinese open-model teams.

Nvidia-backed Reflection AI has released Beam, a mixture-of-experts model aimed at coding and agentic workflows. It has 501 billion total parameters and activates 22 billion per token—an architecture designed to balance capacity and inference efficiency.

For enterprise AI leaders, the important development is not another headline parameter count. It is the growing range of models that can be evaluated, hosted and governed outside a single closed API.

Three questions should come before adoption:
- Do the licence and deployment options meet internal control requirements?
- Do independent evaluations confirm the vendor’s performance claims?
- What are the real serving cost, security and operational-resilience trade-offs?

More choice is valuable only when evaluation and governance keep pace.
