Vertical AI platforms are becoming the next battleground for enterprise adoption.

Google Cloud has announced Gemini Enterprise for Financial Services, with industry input including Deutsche Bank. The product direction is notable: banks do not only need better chat interfaces; they need controls, auditability, data governance and integration with regulated workflows.

The signal for AI leaders is that hyperscalers are moving from generic copilots toward sector-specific agentic platforms. That may accelerate adoption, but it also raises sharper questions about model risk management, vendor concentration and evidence for regulators.

Key takeaways:
- Financial-services AI is becoming more vertical and workflow-specific.
- Controls and auditability are part of the product proposition, not an afterthought.
- Banks should benchmark these offerings against internal governance and architecture standards.
