AI safety coordination now has an antitrust dimension.

Four paid subscribers have filed a proposed class action alleging that Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI and Google illegally coordinated a slowdown in AI development after public discussions on September 12. This is an allegation, not a legal finding, and the companies had not commented when AP published.

The case exposes a real governance tension:

- common safety standards can reduce systemic risk;
- private coordination among direct competitors can trigger competition concerns;
- government-mediated safe harbors may be needed for narrowly scoped safety cooperation.

Banks should watch the distinction between shared assurance standards—which can benefit the whole market—and coordinated commercial behavior. Procurement and industry forums need clear legal guardrails.
