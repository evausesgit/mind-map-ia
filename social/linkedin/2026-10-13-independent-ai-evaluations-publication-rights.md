An AI evaluation is not fully independent if the model provider can determine what the evaluator may publish.

Reuters reports that Anthropic and OpenAI have sought controls over external evaluators’ disclosures, citing trade-secret concerns, even as both support independent review in principle. The underlying issue is a governance gap: there is no common standard for evaluator access, methodology, publication rights and remediation timelines.

Enterprise buyers should not wait for that standard to emerge. Procurement and model-risk frameworks can already ask:
- who designed and funded the evaluation?
- what evidence was withheld or contractually restricted?
- can material negative findings be disclosed without provider approval?

Independent assurance requires both technical access and institutional freedom.
