The next phase of AI regulation will be less about drafting principles and more about proving that controls work.

European Commission Executive Vice-President Henna Virkkunen says the AI Act's lifecycle-based framework is capable of addressing risks from rogue AI agents. Reuters also reports that the Commission has sent information requests to more than 30 AI companies.

For enterprise AI leaders, that points toward an evidence-based operating model:
- maintain traceable model and agent inventories;
- document evaluation, oversight and incident-response controls;
- preserve evidence across the full system lifecycle, including tools and third parties.

Agent governance cannot stop at model approval. It must cover permissions, actions, monitoring and accountability in production.
