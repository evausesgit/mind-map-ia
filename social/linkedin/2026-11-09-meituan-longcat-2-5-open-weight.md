Open-weight competition is moving into large multimodal agent systems.

Meituan’s LongCat 2.5 is a mixture-of-experts model with 1.6 trillion total parameters and about 48 billion active per token. Its weights are available on Hugging Face, targeting long-horizon coding, full-project builds, visual understanding, design and 3D tasks.

For enterprise teams, the release creates both options and obligations:
- more control over hosting and data boundaries;
- potential flexibility on cost and provider dependency;
- responsibility for infrastructure, security testing and model governance.

The useful comparison is not a headline benchmark. It is task-level quality, latency, operating cost, license conditions and safety under the organization’s own harness.
