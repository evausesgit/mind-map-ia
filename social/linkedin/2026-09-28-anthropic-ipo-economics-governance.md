Frontier AI is becoming a public-market test of both economics and governance.

A confidential Anthropic IPO prospectus reviewed by Reuters reportedly shows $4.59 billion in 2025 revenue alongside an operating loss above $8 billion and very large future infrastructure commitments. Separate reporting says founders would retain control through a Founder LLC intended to protect the company’s public-benefit mission.

For enterprise buyers, the key questions extend beyond model quality:

- Can pricing remain stable under this capital intensity?
- How concentrated are cloud and compute dependencies?
- Who ultimately decides when commercial and safety objectives conflict?

Vendor due diligence increasingly needs to cover financial resilience and control structures alongside technical benchmarks.
