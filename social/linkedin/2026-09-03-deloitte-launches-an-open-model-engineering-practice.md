Date: 2026-09-03

Open models are becoming an enterprise implementation market, not just a benchmark race.

Deloitte has announced an Open Model Engineering practice focused on helping organisations adapt, evaluate and govern open models. That is a notable signal: large enterprises are increasingly asking how to operationalise open-weight models safely, not simply whether they can download them.

The practical agenda includes:
- model selection and adaptation;
- evaluation, red-teaming and monitoring;
- licensing, data controls and lifecycle governance.

For AI leaders, this reinforces a broader shift: the value is moving from access to models toward the engineering and governance stack around them.
