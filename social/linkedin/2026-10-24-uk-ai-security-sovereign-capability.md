AI policy is becoming industrial policy, security policy and workforce policy at the same time.

The UK Treasury’s new package combines £165 million for AI biosecurity, £120 million more for the AI Security Institute, £60 million for agentic cyber defence and £70 million for a national vulnerability centre. It also backs domestic chip design and work on how AI will reshape jobs.

For enterprise AI leaders, the useful signal is the package design:
- adoption and risk controls are funded together;
- cyber and biosecurity are treated as operational capabilities;
- strategic dependency on overseas technology is now a board-level policy issue.

Financial institutions should expect this same logic to shape procurement, resilience testing and third-party risk expectations.
