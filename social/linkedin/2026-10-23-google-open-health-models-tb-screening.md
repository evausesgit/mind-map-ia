The most useful AI story is often not a new benchmark. It is a deployment that survives real-world constraints.

Google reports that partner TB-screening tools now cover about 40 facilities in six African countries and have evaluated more than 25,000 chest X-rays, reducing turnaround from weeks to under 45 seconds. Its open-weight MedGemma and MedSigLIP models also support local and offline use, including Indonesia’s plan to screen 50 million people annually while keeping patient data in-country.

The enterprise lessons are broader than healthcare:
- local inference can solve sovereignty and connectivity constraints;
- regulatory certification and workflow integration matter as much as model quality;
- human professionals remain accountable for final decisions.

This is what operational AI maturity looks like: model, infrastructure, governance and domain workflow designed together.
