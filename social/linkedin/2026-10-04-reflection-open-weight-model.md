The next open-weight decision will be about control—not ideology.

Axios reports that Nvidia-backed Reflection is preparing a Western open-weight model aimed at competing with leading systems from Qwen and DeepSeek. The promise is attractive for enterprises that want to combine proprietary data, controlled infrastructure and lower-cost customisation.

But the decisive evidence is still missing:
- the licence and permitted commercial uses;
- independently reproducible evaluations;
- security, provenance and patching commitments.

Open weights can reduce API dependency. They also transfer more validation and operational responsibility to the adopter. That trade-off matters especially in banking and capital markets.
