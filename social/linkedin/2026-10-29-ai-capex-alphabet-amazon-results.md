The market is becoming less patient with AI investment that cannot yet show operating leverage.

Alphabet shares fell after a revenue miss, while Amazon also came under pressure as expanding AI expenditure overshadowed strong cloud growth. The signal is broader than two earnings reports: capital markets are raising the bar for the AI investment thesis.

Enterprise AI portfolios should expect the same questions:

- Is usage translating into revenue, productivity or avoided risk?
- What is the fully loaded cost per successful task?
- Which workloads deserve premium models and which should be routed to cheaper options?

AI strategy is increasingly a unit-economics discipline, not only a technology roadmap.
