Training-data partnerships need the same transparency as model deployments.

Internal Oxford documents show that out-of-copyright Bodleian Library material digitised through an OpenAI partnership was used in OpenAI’s training set. By June 2025, 125,000 dissertation images had been shared; Oxford says the arrangement is modest, non-exclusive and will also produce openly available scans.

Why this matters:

- digitisation rights and model-training rights should be stated separately;
- provenance records can become strategic assets for governance and evaluation;
- institutional partners need reputational, environmental and public-interest review.

As high-quality human-created data becomes scarcer, data contracts will increasingly shape both capability and trust.
