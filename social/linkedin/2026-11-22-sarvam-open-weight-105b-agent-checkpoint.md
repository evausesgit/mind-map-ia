Open-weight models are moving closer to the operational constraints of enterprise agents.

Sarvam has released Sarvam-105B-1711, a mixture-of-experts checkpoint with 105 billion total parameters and 10.3 billion active parameters. It is available in BF16 and as a 65 GB NVFP4 build designed to fit on one 96 GB workstation GPU.

Three points stand out:
- stronger vendor-reported tool-use and instruction-following results;
- a stated blended price of $0.15 per million tokens;
- a credible path to private deployment for sensitive workloads.

The benchmark claims still need independent, workload-specific validation. But the direction matters: open weights are increasingly competing on deployability, agent consistency and unit economics—not only headline benchmark scores.
