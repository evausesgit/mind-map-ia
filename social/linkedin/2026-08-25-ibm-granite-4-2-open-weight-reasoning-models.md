Open-weight AI keeps moving into enterprise territory. IBM's Granite 4.2 release adds Apache-2.0 reasoning models in 3B, 8B, and 30B sizes, with tool calling and agentic evaluation at the center of the release.

This matters because many organizations want more than a black-box API for sensitive workflows. They need inspectability, deployment flexibility, cost control, and licensing clarity — especially when models touch code, documents, or regulated business processes.

The practical signal: open-weight models are no longer only a research or hobbyist option. They are becoming part of the enterprise architecture discussion alongside frontier APIs.
