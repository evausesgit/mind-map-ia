Time-series foundation models are becoming a practical part of the open-model ecosystem.

IBM has released Granite Time Series PatchTST-FM-r2, a roughly 385M-parameter model for zero-shot forecasting. Its weights, architecture, inference pipeline, and reproducible benchmark code are available under commercially friendly Apache 2.0 and OpenMDW 1.0 licenses.

Notable points:
- second overall among replicable zero-shot models on GIFT-Eval;
- first in that category among permissively licensed models;
- support for probabilistic forecasts, missing-value imputation, and contexts up to 8,192 steps.

For enterprises, the value is not a leaderboard alone. Open artifacts make it possible to test forecasting on internal distributions, document limitations, and deploy under tighter data and model controls.
