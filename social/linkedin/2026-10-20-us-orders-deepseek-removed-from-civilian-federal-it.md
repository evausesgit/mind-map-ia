AI governance is becoming a supply-chain discipline, not just a usage policy.

The US Department of Homeland Security has ordered DeepSeek products removed from civilian federal IT and excluded them from covered procurement. The scope includes affiliated entities and certain third-party offerings built on the technology.

For enterprise AI leaders, three questions move to the front:
- Can we identify the model lineage behind every AI-enabled service?
- Can procurement enforce exclusions across indirect dependencies?
- Can we replace a model quickly without breaking critical workflows?

Open weights can improve control and portability, but they do not eliminate geopolitical, provenance or compliance risk.
