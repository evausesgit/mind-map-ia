Open-weight AI competition is becoming a serious enterprise option, not a side discussion.

SCMP reports that DeepSeek is leading renewed attention to low-cost Chinese open-weight models on a major US model platform. This sits alongside the broader momentum around Qwen, Z.ai, and other Chinese labs.

Why it matters:
- cost and latency pressure on proprietary APIs will keep rising;
- model governance must cover licensing, provenance, evaluation, and geopolitical risk;
- banks will need clear rules for where open-weight deployment is acceptable.

The practical question is no longer whether open-weight models are useful. It is how to evaluate them rigorously enough for enterprise controls.
