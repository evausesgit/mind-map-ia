The most credible case for AI in cybersecurity may be the continuous defensive loop: find, reproduce, patch and retest.

OpenAI is preparing another internal security sprint with Cathedral Systems. Its previous exercise involved 25% of product engineers, with Codex used to investigate findings, reproduce vulnerabilities in isolated environments and prepare patches for human review.

OpenAI is also:
- more than quadrupling bug-bounty rewards for critical findings;
- committing $1 billion in subsidized access over six months for frontline defenders;
- sharing elements of its defensive architecture and lessons.

For enterprise AI leaders, the message is not to automate security blindly. It is to redesign the operating model so AI-assisted discovery feeds controlled remediation, validation and accountability — faster than attackers can exploit the same capabilities.
