Open-weight models are increasingly part of enterprise AI strategy. A new security warning from University of Waterloo researchers is a useful reminder: availability is not the same as readiness for production.

For regulated organizations, the practical question is not whether open models are valuable. They clearly are. The question is how to wrap them in evaluation, provenance and runtime controls that match the risk of each use case.

Key takeaways for AI teams:
- Treat open-weight model intake like third-party software and data supply chain review.
- Add security-specific red teaming before production use, not only accuracy benchmarks.
- Keep monitoring after deployment, because model behavior depends heavily on prompts, tools and context.

Open models can reduce dependency risk and enable local deployment, but they need bank-grade governance to be safely useful.
