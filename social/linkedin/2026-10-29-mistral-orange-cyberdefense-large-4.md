Open weights are becoming an enterprise deployment strategy, not just a research distribution choice.

Mistral says Orange Cyberdefense is testing a specialised Large 4 variant for authorised penetration testing, vulnerability assessment and red-teaming, with vetting and contractual safeguards.

The interesting point is the operating model:
- sovereign deployment and customisation;
- domain-specific optimisation;
- explicit controls for dual-use capability.

For regulated firms, this is a useful pattern: capability, deployment control and contractual governance need to be designed together. Vendor benchmarks still require independent validation in the target environment.
