Agentic AI in banking is becoming a resilience question, not only a model-risk question.

Reuters reports that US and European supervisors are asking banks how autonomous agents are bounded, monitored, approved and shut down. The scrutiny also covers foreign-developed models, third-party dependencies, clean recovery environments and the ability to reconstruct agent decisions.

Three practical implications for AI leaders:
- agent identity, permissions and action logs need the rigor of privileged-access management;
- recovery tests must assume that primary systems and AI tooling are both compromised;
- inventories must connect models, agents, tools, data and external providers.

The governance test is shifting from “was the model approved?” to “can the institution detect, contain and recover from an agent acting at machine speed?”
