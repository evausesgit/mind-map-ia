The AI industry is no longer aligned on whether frontier development should slow down collectively.

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg argues that competition and potential liability already give each lab enough incentive to invest in alignment and safety. His position contrasts with calls from Dario Amodei, Sam Altman and Elon Musk for a coordinated slowdown as model capabilities and misuse risks rise.

For enterprise leaders, the disagreement is a reminder that vendor assurances are not a substitute for internal controls:
- define risk acceptance independently of release cadence;
- require evidence for safety and security claims;
- preserve the ability to pause or switch models.

The governance question is moving from “are models safe?” to “who decides what level of risk is acceptable, and who remains accountable?”
