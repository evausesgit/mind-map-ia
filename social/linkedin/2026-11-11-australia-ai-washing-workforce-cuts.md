“AI-driven restructuring” needs evidence, not a label.

Australian unions and experts are questioning companies that cite AI when announcing job cuts while also moving work offshore. The concern is that automation claims may blur the respective impact of technology, outsourcing, and conventional cost reduction.

Responsible workforce transformation should measure:
- which tasks were actually automated;
- which roles were redesigned or relocated;
- what productivity gains were realised;
- how employees were retrained or redeployed.

For AI leaders, this is a governance issue as much as a communications issue. Unsupported claims can damage trust with employees, regulators, and investors.
