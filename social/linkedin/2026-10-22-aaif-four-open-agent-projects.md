The open agent stack is starting to look like infrastructure, not a collection of demos.

The Agentic AI Foundation has added four projects contributed by Anthropic, OpenAI, Hugging Face and Alibaba: Agent Skills, Open Responses, OpenEnv and OpenSandbox. Together they address portable capabilities, multi-provider model interfaces, reinforcement-learning environments and secure execution.

For enterprise builders, open governance could reduce integration friction and vendor lock-in. But “open” does not remove the need for:
- versioned compatibility and conformance testing;
- security review of sandboxes and third-party skills;
- internal standards for identity, policy and audit evidence.

Interoperability is becoming a strategic control point in agent architecture.
