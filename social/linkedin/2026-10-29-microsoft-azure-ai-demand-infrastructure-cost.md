Enterprise AI demand is growing, but so is the bill for supplying it.

Microsoft’s latest quarterly results again point to strong Azure and AI demand while keeping the scale of infrastructure investment at the centre of the investor debate.

For enterprise AI leaders, the signal is two-sided:
- adoption is moving deeper into production;
- capacity and unit economics remain constraints;
- concentration on a small number of cloud and model providers is a strategic risk.

This argues for workload-level cost measurement, caching, model routing and realistic exit plans—not simply a bigger central AI budget.
