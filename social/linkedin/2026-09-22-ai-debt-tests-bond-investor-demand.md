The AI infrastructure cycle is becoming a credit-market story, not only an equity-market story.

BNP Paribas data cited by Reuters shows that AI hyperscalers issued $220 billion of debt in 2026 through August 10, compared with $12.5 billion over the equivalent period last year. Investors still view the largest issuers as strong credits, but they are asking for more yield to absorb repeated, long-dated supply.

Recent signals include:
- Amazon’s $25 billion long-dated sale priced at roughly 120 basis points over Treasuries.
- Technology spreads reached 89 basis points, nine wider than the overall investment-grade market.
- Portfolio concentration limits at pension funds and insurers may constrain demand even before credit fundamentals deteriorate.

For AI leaders, this changes the economics of the roadmap. Compute commitments, model efficiency and measurable business value increasingly affect financing costs and capital allocation — not just technology budgets.
