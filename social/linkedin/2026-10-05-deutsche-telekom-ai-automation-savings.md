The enterprise AI conversation is becoming more measurable.

Deutsche Telekom expects AI and automation to cut about €2.5 billion in indirect costs by 2030 compared with 2023. That is a notable public target from a large, complex operator—and a useful reference point for organisations trying to move beyond pilot counts.

A credible value programme should track more than gross savings:
- benefits realised versus forecast;
- service quality and operational incidents;
- implementation, model and change-management costs;
- workforce redesign and control effectiveness.

AI value is not the size of a target announced today. It is the durable, risk-adjusted outcome evidenced over time.
