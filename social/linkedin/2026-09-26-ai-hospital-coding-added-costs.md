AI can optimise each side of a process while making the overall system more expensive.

A Blue Cross Blue Shield Association analysis links AI-assisted hospital claims coding to $942 million in additional US healthcare spending over two years. It found more patients classified as having complex conditions without evidence of corresponding changes in treatment; hospitals dispute that interpretation.

The case highlights three governance questions:

- which metric is the model really optimising?
- can outcomes be validated against the underlying service delivered?
- what happens when agents on both sides learn to contest one another?

The broader lesson is to measure system-level outcomes, not only local productivity gains.
