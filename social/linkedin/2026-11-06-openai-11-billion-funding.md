OpenAI has reportedly closed an initial $11 billion portion of a funding round at a $1.25 trillion valuation. The headline is extraordinary, but the enterprise implication is more grounded: model roadmaps increasingly depend on sustained access to capital, energy, chips and data-center execution.

AI leaders should evaluate vendors across three dimensions:
- capability and price today;
- financial and infrastructure durability;
- portability if commercial terms or strategic priorities change.

A strong model is not, by itself, a complete supplier-risk strategy.
