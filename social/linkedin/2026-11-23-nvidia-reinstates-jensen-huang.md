Nvidia’s leadership reversal is a reminder that key-person risk can become market infrastructure risk.

Jensen Huang has been reinstated as CEO after US government intervention. Nvidia shares rose 31%, adding nearly $1 trillion in market value, while the Commerce Department separately moved toward blacklisting Megaspeed over suspected export-control violations.

The rally does not close the governance questions:
- congressional and regulatory investigations remain open;
- export controls are now intertwined with supplier and customer due diligence;
- the market’s dependence on one company—and one leader—has become unusually visible.

For banks and large AI buyers, concentration analysis should include leadership continuity, regulatory exposure and geopolitical dependencies alongside compute capacity and price.
