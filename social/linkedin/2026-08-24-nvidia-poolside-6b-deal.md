The AI stack is becoming more vertically strategic.

Reports that Nvidia is exploring an approximately $6B agreement with Poolside are another sign that compute suppliers, model labs, and enterprise demand are becoming tightly linked.

This matters for AI strategy because infrastructure decisions are no longer only technical procurement choices. They influence model access, cost curves, vendor concentration, and long-term portability.

Enterprise AI leaders should keep pressure on three dimensions:
- cost per useful task, not just token price;
- portability across cloud, GPU, and model providers;
- governance of strategic supplier dependencies.

The frontier-model race is increasingly a capital, compute, and distribution race.
