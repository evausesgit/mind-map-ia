Open-weight AI needs an open deployment stack, not only downloadable weights.

DeepSeek has released DeepInfer, the high-performance inference framework used to serve its models at scale. This opens another layer of the stack to inspection and adaptation.

For enterprise teams, the opportunity comes with a qualification checklist:
- benchmark total cost against alternatives such as vLLM and SGLang;
- validate hardware support, observability and operational resilience;
- treat dependencies, patches and build provenance as software-supply-chain risk.

Openness can reduce vendor dependency, but only if the operating model is mature enough to own the additional responsibility.
