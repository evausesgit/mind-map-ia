A provider’s risk appetite is not an enterprise risk appetite.

Sam Altman has argued that broad access to powerful AI justifies accepting some harmful outcomes, while drawing the line at catastrophic risks. That is a consequential policy position—but regulated organisations still have to define their own boundaries.

For AI leaders, this means making three things explicit:
- which failure modes are tolerable for each use case;
- who can pause or withdraw a system;
- what evidence and contractual controls are required from providers.

Innovation depends on access. Trust depends on measurable controls, independent evaluation and accountability when things go wrong.
