Open-weight AI is increasingly a geopolitical and infrastructure story, not only a model-quality story.

CNBC reports that Nvidia is expanding support for Chinese open AI models such as Qwen and DeepSeek, while warning that tighter restrictions could accelerate fragmentation of AI ecosystems. This matters because model adoption, hardware optimisation and developer tooling often reinforce each other.

For enterprises, the practical question is becoming more nuanced: which open models are acceptable, where can they run, what licences and security reviews apply, and how exposed is the deployment stack to geopolitical constraints?

Key takeaways:
- Chinese open-weight models remain important in the global developer ecosystem.
- Hardware vendors have incentives to support broad model adoption.
- Governance teams need clear policies for open-model evaluation, provenance and deployment.
