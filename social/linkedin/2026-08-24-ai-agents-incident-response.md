AI agents change the shape of incident response.

The failure mode is no longer only “a human account was compromised.” It can also be “an authorized agent made the wrong decision, triggered an action, or exposed data.”

That distinction matters. Agentic systems need controls that are designed for machine-initiated access, not retrofitted from human workflows.

A practical control model includes:
- dedicated agent identities;
- least-privilege tool access;
- human approval for sensitive actions;
- kill switches and rollback paths;
- audit logs that explain who, what, when, and why.

If agents are going to operate inside enterprise workflows, incident response must become agent-aware by design.
