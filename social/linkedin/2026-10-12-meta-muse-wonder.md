Meta is back in the frontier-model conversation — but this time through proprietary agent models rather than open weights.

Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full target personal and professional agents, memory-heavy workflows, and long-running coding tasks. Meta reports leading results on several agentic and coding evaluations. Those are useful signals, but enterprise teams should still demand independent tests, operating-cost data, and evidence on reliability under real tool use.

Three questions matter now:
- How stable are the models across long, multi-step workflows?
- What controls exist for permissions, memory, and auditability?
- Will Meta connect this proprietary line to its wider open-model ecosystem?

For AI leaders, benchmark leadership is only the beginning. Governability and predictable execution will decide whether these systems reach production.
