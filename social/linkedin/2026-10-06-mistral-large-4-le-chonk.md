Europe’s open-weight model landscape may be about to gain a much larger contender.

Mistral Large 4, nicknamed “Le Chonk”, is a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model with about 1.05 trillion total parameters and roughly 49–52 billion active parameters. It is in public preview, with downloadable weights announced for the end of October.

Before enterprise adoption, three points need validation:
- the final licence and permitted commercial uses;
- infrastructure requirements and total serving cost;
- independent quality, safety and multilingual evaluations.

Open weights can improve deployment control and data sovereignty. They do not remove governance, security or lifecycle obligations — especially at this scale.
