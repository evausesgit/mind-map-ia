Open-weight AI is expanding beyond language into physical-world simulation.

Nvidia has released four 16-billion-parameter Cosmos 3 Nano checkpoints for interactive world simulation under the OpenMDW 1.1 licence. The family covers driving-scene transfer, streaming rollouts, and robotics simulation.

Why this matters:
- synthetic environments can accelerate testing where real data is costly or dangerous;
- open weights support local evaluation and adaptation;
- licences, safety assumptions, and simulation-to-reality gaps still require careful review.

The open-model conversation is becoming broader: not only who controls language models, but who can inspect and adapt the world models used for autonomous systems.
