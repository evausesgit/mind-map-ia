The most dangerous security window is often the one before a patch can be deployed.

OpenAI and Cisco have introduced Shirudo, an open-source infrastructure shield for Linux systems that cannot be patched immediately. It targets the operational gap between vulnerability disclosure, remediation and rollout.

- Compensating controls can reduce exposure during patch delays.
- Open source allows inspection and adaptation.
- Deployment governance remains as important as detection quality.

For enterprise AI and cyber teams, the value is practical: shorten the period in which known vulnerabilities remain exploitable while preserving normal change controls.
