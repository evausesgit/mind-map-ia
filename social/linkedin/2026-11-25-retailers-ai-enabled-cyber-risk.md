AI-enabled cyber risk is becoming a balance-sheet issue.

Reuters reports that retailers face a rising threat ahead of Black Friday while many remain uninsured against the resulting losses. Faster reconnaissance and attack automation collide with peak transaction volumes, third-party dependencies and limited risk transfer.

The practical priorities are familiar but more urgent:
- harden privileged and machine identities;
- rehearse incident response under peak load;
- test supplier and payment-chain resilience.

Organizations should not treat AI cyber risk as a model-policy topic alone. It belongs in operational resilience, insurance and executive risk governance.
