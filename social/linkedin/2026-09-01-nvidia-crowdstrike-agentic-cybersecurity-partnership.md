Agentic AI is entering cybersecurity through detection, triage and response workflows.

NVIDIA and CrowdStrike announced an expanded collaboration around agentic cybersecurity at Fal.Con 2026, combining NVIDIA AI infrastructure with CrowdStrike’s security platform.

The enterprise implication is practical:
- security teams will need policies for when agents can recommend, escalate or act;
- model and tool permissions become part of cyber control design;
- SOC productivity gains must be balanced against automation risk.

This is a good example of where “AI agents” stop being a demo category and become a control, liability and operating-model question.
