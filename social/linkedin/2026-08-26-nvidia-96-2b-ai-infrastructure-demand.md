Nvidia's latest quarter is another reminder that AI is no longer just a software story. It is an infrastructure cycle with direct consequences for enterprise roadmaps, budgets, and concentration risk.

The company reported $96.2B in quarterly revenue, up 106% year over year, with Data Center revenue at $89.0B. That level of growth reflects sustained demand for training and inference capacity, but also raises practical questions about vendor dependency, power, deployment lead times, and cost discipline.

For AI leaders in regulated industries, the takeaway is not simply "buy more GPUs". It is to connect model strategy with infrastructure governance:
- where proprietary APIs are enough,
- where dedicated capacity is justified,
- and where open-weight or smaller models can reduce cost and dependency.
