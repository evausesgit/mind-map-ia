Open models are not only about chatbots.

Hugging Face published a technical post on Granite Speech 5.0 Turbo CTC, focused on fast and accurate speech-to-text workflows. Transcription is becoming a foundational capability for meeting capture, contact-centre analytics, surveillance review and multilingual knowledge systems.

Why this matters for builders:
- open speech models can reduce vendor lock-in;
- deployment choices affect privacy and latency;
- evaluation needs to include accents, domain vocabulary and compliance constraints.

For regulated enterprises, speech AI should be governed as part of the broader data and model supply chain.
