Smaller open-weight models can be strategically useful when the task is narrow and data sensitivity is high.

Xiaomi-OCR-0 is a 0.8B vision-language model derived from Qwen3.5-0.8B and trained on roughly 170 million OCR-focused samples. Its maintainers report strong results for document parsing, tables and formulas, with local deployment paths and MCP tooling.

For enterprise document workflows, the opportunity is clear:
- lower-cost, private inference;
- task-specific evaluation instead of frontier-model overkill;
- more control over latency and data residency.

The caveat: published benchmarks are not production validation. Multilingual documents, irregular layouts, licence terms and operational security still need independent testing.
