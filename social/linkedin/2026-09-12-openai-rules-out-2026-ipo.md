OpenAI will not pursue an IPO in 2026, according to Sam Altman, as scrutiny of advanced-model safety intensifies.

An IPO is more than a financing event. It changes disclosure obligations, quarterly incentives and the balance between rapid commercialization and long-horizon risk management. Delaying it leaves those trade-offs inside a private governance structure for longer.

Enterprise customers should still press for the evidence they would expect from a critical supplier:
- transparent incident reporting;
- clear model-change and deprecation policies;
- auditable controls for autonomous capabilities.

Vendor financial structure does not remove third-party risk. It changes how customers need to obtain assurance.
