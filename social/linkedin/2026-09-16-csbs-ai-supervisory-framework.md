AI governance is becoming examinable banking evidence.

The Conference of State Bank Supervisors has published a risk-based framework for state examiners reviewing AI at banks and nonbanks. It covers governance, inventories, generative and agentic systems, third-party providers, model risk and consumer protection.

The framework does not create new legal obligations, but it gives supervisors a common way to ask whether institutions can:
- identify material AI use cases and accountable owners;
- explain risk tiering and controls;
- evidence ongoing oversight of vendors and customer impacts.

For financial institutions, a current AI inventory linked to controls and documentary evidence is becoming the minimum viable foundation for supervisory readiness.
