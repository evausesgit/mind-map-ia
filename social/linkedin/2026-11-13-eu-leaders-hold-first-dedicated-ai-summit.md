Europe’s AI challenge is no longer only about writing rules. It is about turning them into industrial capacity.

EU leaders have held their first summit devoted exclusively to AI, with competitiveness, compute, energy, skills, sovereignty and safety all on the agenda. The strategic question is whether Europe can connect regulation with deployment at scale.

For enterprise AI leaders, three issues stand out:
- access to reliable compute and energy;
- a workable governance path from pilot to production;
- a stronger European supplier and skills ecosystem.

The next test will be execution: shared infrastructure, investment and procurement—not another layer of principles.
