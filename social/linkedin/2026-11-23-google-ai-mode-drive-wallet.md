The next competitive edge for AI assistants may be privileged access to personal and enterprise context.

Google is extending Personal Intelligence in Search AI Mode to optional connections with Drive, Wallet and Gemini. Wallet access is read-only and users can switch the feature off, but the product direction is clear: assistants are moving closer to documents, transactions and financial decisions.

For regulated organizations, similar integrations require more than consent screens:
- purpose limitation and least-privilege access;
- visible provenance for the context used in each answer;
- retention, revocation and audit controls that work across connected systems.

Personalization can improve relevance, but it also expands the blast radius of a wrong answer, compromised account or overly broad permission.
