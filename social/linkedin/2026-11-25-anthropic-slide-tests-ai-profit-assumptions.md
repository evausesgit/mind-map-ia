Public markets are putting AI economics under a much harder test.

Reuters says Anthropic’s post-IPO slide is challenging Wall Street’s assumptions about future AI profits as the broader sector selloff continues. The debate is shifting from adoption and capability to capital intensity, revenue quality and sustainable margins.

For enterprise AI leaders, this matters beyond equity prices. Vendor resilience, pricing stability and infrastructure commitments all depend on credible unit economics.

The strategic response is not to stop investing—it is to measure value by workload, preserve provider portability and avoid commitments based only on headline model performance.
