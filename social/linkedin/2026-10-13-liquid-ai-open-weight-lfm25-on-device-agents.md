The open-weight model race is increasingly about where inference can run, not only how models rank.

Liquid AI has released LFM2.5-24B-A2B for local agent workloads. The mixture-of-experts model has 24B total parameters with roughly 2B active, and the company reports 184 tokens per second on an Apple M5 Max and 114 on an AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395.

That makes three evaluation dimensions especially important:
- whether local deployment reduces latency and data exposure in practice;
- whether tool use and reasoning quality hold up on real workflows;
- whether the license, observability and security posture fit enterprise controls.

Open weights can improve portability and vendor leverage, but they transfer more responsibility for testing, patching and runtime security to the adopter.
