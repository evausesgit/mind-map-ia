European banks are moving from isolated AI pilots to governed, multi-model platforms.

Crédit Agricole is working with Mistral to add European model capabilities to its enterprise AI platform. The strategic value is not simply access to another LLM; it is the option to combine sovereignty, data controls and model choice within a common operating layer.

What will determine success:
- continuous evaluation and routing by use case;
- clear accountability, security and human-review controls;
- measured adoption, unit cost and business outcomes.

A bank-grade AI platform should make models replaceable while keeping governance consistent.
