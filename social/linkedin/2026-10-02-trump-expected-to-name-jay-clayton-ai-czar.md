US AI policy may be moving even closer to the national-security agenda.

Multiple reports say President Donald Trump is expected to name Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton as his top AI adviser. The appointment had not been formally confirmed at the time of reporting.

If confirmed, AI leaders should watch three areas:
- stronger coupling between AI policy, intelligence and cyber risk;
- possible changes to export controls and frontier-model oversight;
- implications for firms operating across US, EU and UK regulatory regimes.

Governance teams should treat this as a policy signal, not yet a settled rule change.
