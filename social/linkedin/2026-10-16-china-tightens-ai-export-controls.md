AI export controls are expanding beyond chips.

Reuters reports that Beijing is tightening oversight of advanced AI models, agents and related know-how. That turns model weights, orchestration software and technical expertise into strategic assets whose cross-border movement may require the same attention as hardware.

For enterprise AI leaders, three implications stand out:
- map where weights and agent components originate;
- include export-control checks in procurement and deployment;
- plan for regional substitutes if access changes.

The next phase of AI geopolitics will be fought across the full stack, not only semiconductors.
