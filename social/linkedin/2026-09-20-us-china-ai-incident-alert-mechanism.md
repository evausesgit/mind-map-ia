AI incident reporting may be entering the realm of strategic diplomacy.

The United States has proposed a notification mechanism with China for AI incidents that could affect national security. The proposal is early-stage: no public scope, thresholds, or enforcement process have been defined.

For regulated enterprises, the signal matters even before an agreement exists:

- incident taxonomies may increasingly need to work across jurisdictions;
- escalation criteria should distinguish operational failures from national-security events;
- evidence and audit trails must support fast, credible notification.

A useful model will require concrete definitions, protected channels, and verification—not just political intent.
