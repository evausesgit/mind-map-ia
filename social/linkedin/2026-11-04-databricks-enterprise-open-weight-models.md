Enterprise model strategy is shifting from “pick one provider” to “govern a portfolio.”

Databricks reports that the average number of models used by its customers rose from 4.2 to 7.1 during 2026, with roughly 40% open-weight. It also found that the same model scored 61.7%, 48.1% and 38.4% under different harnesses—evidence that prompts, tools and control flow can matter as much as model choice.

For AI leaders, the practical lessons are clear:
- evaluate model and harness together on real workflows;
- use routing and fallback policies rather than hard-coded provider choices;
- preserve common controls for access, spend, logging and data retention.

Open-weight models can improve sovereignty, economics and resilience. But their enterprise advantage comes from disciplined portfolio governance, not openness alone.
