Sovereign AI is moving from policy language into banking architecture.

Emirates NBD says it will run OpenAI GPT models on infrastructure located inside the UAE, a first for a MENA bank. The planned use cases span customer engagement, risk and compliance analytics, knowledge workflows and employee productivity.

In-country inference can address an important layer of data-residency risk, but it does not close the governance case by itself. Banks still need controls for model updates, access, logging, retention, human accountability and cross-border support operations.

The practical benchmark for regulated AI is becoming clear: capable models, deployable under local data, resilience and supervisory constraints.
