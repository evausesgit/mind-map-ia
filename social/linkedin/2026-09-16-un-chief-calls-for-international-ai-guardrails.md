The debate over advanced AI risk is becoming a debate over who has authority to set the rules.

UN Secretary-General António Guterres has argued that increasingly capable systems create cross-border risks that voluntary commitments alone cannot manage. His position contrasts sharply with the Trump administration’s dismissal of prominent safety warnings.

For enterprise AI leaders, the practical issue is fragmentation:
- global firms may face incompatible national expectations;
- internal controls may need to exceed the weakest legal baseline;
- evidence from testing, monitoring and incident response will matter more than policy statements.

Regulatory uncertainty is not a reason to wait. It is a reason to build controls that can be explained across jurisdictions.
