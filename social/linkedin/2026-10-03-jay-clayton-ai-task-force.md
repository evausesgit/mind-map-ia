AI policy in the United States is moving closer to the national-security apparatus.

President Donald Trump has appointed Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton as AI czar, according to a Wall Street Journal interview reported by Reuters. The new White House task force is expected to report within 120 days on AI risks and opportunities.

For enterprise AI leaders, three questions now matter:
- Will the review reshape model-security or export-control requirements?
- How will US policy diverge from the EU and UK frameworks?
- What new evidence will global firms need from strategic AI suppliers?

The title is political; the operational impact will be in controls, procurement and cross-border deployment.
