Enterprise AI is moving from generic copilots toward industry-specific implementation packages. Devoteam and Google Cloud are positioning Gemini Enterprise for financial services and legal workflows, where controls and integration matter as much as model quality.

For regulated sectors, the value proposition is less about a chatbot and more about managed workflows: data boundaries, permissions, auditability, evaluation and integration into existing processes.

What to watch:
- Whether vertical AI platforms reduce time-to-production for regulated use cases.
- How agentic workflows are governed when they touch sensitive data or operational systems.
- Whether partners can provide reusable patterns rather than one-off proofs of concept.

The winners in enterprise AI may be the teams that industrialize governance and integration, not only prompt design.
