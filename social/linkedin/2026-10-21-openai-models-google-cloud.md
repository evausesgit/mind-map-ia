Multi-model enterprise AI just became easier to buy — but not automatically easier to govern.

Google Cloud is bringing OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, plus Codex workflows, into Model Garden. Customers can use Google Cloud Marketplace billing, existing cloud commitments, unified identity and audit controls.

For AI leaders, three questions remain:
- Which controls are available now, versus merely announced?
- How portable are evaluations, prompts and controls across models?
- Does vendor choice reduce lock-in, or move it to the cloud platform?

The strongest operating model will keep model selection dynamic while standardising evidence, access controls and risk acceptance.
