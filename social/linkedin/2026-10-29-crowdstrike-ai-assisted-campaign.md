AI is changing the economics of cyber operations faster than it is changing the underlying attack techniques.

CrowdStrike says a China-linked group used an agentic framework and models including DeepSeek to target more than 200 organisations in 11 days. The important signal is scale: reconnaissance, adaptation and exploitation can be compressed into shorter cycles.

For security and AI leaders:
- defensive telemetry must operate at comparable speed;
- model and agent usage should be included in threat intelligence;
- vendor attribution and performance claims still require evidence-based review.

The enterprise response should focus on identity, segmentation, patching and rapid containment—not on treating “AI attack” as a new category detached from existing controls.
