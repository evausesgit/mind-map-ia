Chat interfaces are becoming business distribution channels.

OpenAI is launching Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, allowing companies to claim verified profiles, update information, and submit product feeds with current prices and availability. Eligible US e-commerce companies can also create Business Agents.

This creates a new operating layer for enterprises:
- structured data quality becomes customer-facing;
- agent behaviour needs brand, legal, and conduct controls;
- attribution, ranking, and transaction governance become strategic issues.

The important shift is from “being mentioned by an assistant” to actively managing how an AI channel represents and serves a business.
