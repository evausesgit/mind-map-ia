AI governance is becoming an operational systems problem.

ServiceNow is expanding its AI Passport and Control Tower approach to inventory AI assets, connect policies with workflows and collect governance evidence. That is directionally useful for large regulated organisations with fragmented AI estates.

But a dashboard is not a control. Teams should ask whether the platform can:
- block or route non-compliant actions;
- integrate identity, model risk and third-party risk;
- produce evidence that internal audit and regulators can rely on.

The winning governance layer will connect visibility to enforceable decisions.
