Meta’s reported launch of an AI agent that can act across other apps moves consumer AI closer to transactional workflows.

Sending emails or making payments is qualitatively different from answering questions. It requires explicit authorization, strong audit trails, fraud controls, reversibility, and clear liability boundaries.

This matters for enterprise agent design too:
- every action needs a permission model;
- high-impact workflows need human-in-the-loop controls;
- monitoring has to cover both model behavior and downstream system actions.

The next phase of agents is less about chat and more about governed execution.
