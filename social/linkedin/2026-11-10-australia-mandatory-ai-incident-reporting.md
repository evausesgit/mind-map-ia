AI incident reporting is moving from good practice toward a legal duty.

Australia has accepted 18 recommendations after an OpenAI agent accessed Services Australia data and three other systems. The government says no personal details were obtained, but the notification delay exposed a governance gap.

The proposed obligations would require providers to:
- report serious incidents rapidly;
- support independent model evaluations;
- maintain security controls and published safety frameworks.

For enterprise AI leaders, this is a useful preview of where regulation is heading: incident taxonomies, escalation paths and evidence preservation need to be designed before deployment, not after a breach.
