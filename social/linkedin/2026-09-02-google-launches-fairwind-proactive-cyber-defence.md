Cyber defence is moving further upstream: Google has introduced Fairwind, a programme aimed at helping governments and enterprises act before attacks materialise.

The important part is not just another security product announcement. It is the framing: AI-assisted threat intelligence, vulnerability discovery and response practices are becoming part of the operating model for large organisations.

For regulated enterprises, this raises practical questions:
- how AI-discovered cyber gaps are prioritised against remediation capacity;
- how evidence, escalation and audit trails are preserved;
- how security teams govern agentic or semi-autonomous response workflows.

The direction is clear: AI will not only increase attacker productivity; it will also reshape defensive workflows and cyber-risk governance.
