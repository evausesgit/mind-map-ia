AI governance is becoming operational, not theoretical.

New York has opened registration for frontier AI developers under the RAISE Act. Companies above the law's $500 million revenue threshold must register by January 1, publish safety frameworks and transparency reports, and report critical incidents within 72 hours.

For enterprise AI leaders, three implications stand out:
- incident taxonomies and escalation paths need to exist before deployment;
- evidence retention must support a 72-hour reporting clock;
- vendor due diligence should test whether providers can meet multiple state regimes.

This is another reason to treat model governance, third-party risk and operational resilience as one control system rather than separate workstreams.
