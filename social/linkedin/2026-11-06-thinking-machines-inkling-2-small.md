A new open-weight contender is targeting long-horizon enterprise work.

Thinking Machines introduced Inkling 2 Small, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 594B total parameters and 17B active. It is available through Tinker with closed weights today, with full Apache-2.0 weights planned next week after external safety testing and red-teaming.

The company reports strong results on enterprise and finance-oriented tasks, but the important operational test starts after release:
- reproducibility outside the vendor environment;
- deployment cost and latency;
- security behavior after fine-tuning;
- license and control fit for regulated use.

Open weights widen strategic options, but they also move more assurance responsibility onto the deployer.
