Strong IPO demand does not remove the hard questions around AI economics.

Reuters reports that institutional interest in Anthropic’s planned listing is outpacing the early stage of SpaceX’s roadshow. That is a meaningful signal of investor appetite — but the more useful discussion is about durability.

Investors will need to test:
- whether model differentiation can survive rapid frontier-model cycles;
- whether enterprise growth can offset compute-intensive margins;
- how dependency on cloud and chip partners affects strategic control.

For enterprise AI leaders, public-market scrutiny may bring more transparency on unit economics, concentration risk and long-term infrastructure commitments.
