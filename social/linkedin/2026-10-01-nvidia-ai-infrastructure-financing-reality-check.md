AI compute is becoming a financing question, not only a technology question.

Reuters reports that Nvidia’s plan to support up to $500 billion of AI infrastructure financing is meeting investor scepticism over the useful life and residual value of chips used as collateral. Rapid hardware depreciation complicates a structure that depends on durable asset values and predictable utilisation.

For financial institutions, the key issues are:
- conservative collateral haircuts and refresh assumptions;
- refinancing and utilisation risk across data-centre vehicles;
- concentration exposure to a small number of vendors and buyers.

The AI build-out may be strategic, but its capital structure still needs ordinary credit discipline.
