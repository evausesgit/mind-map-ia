AI changes the cyber equation on both sides.

A CNBC-reported initiative brings together 116 companies and entities calling for a faster, coordinated cyber-defence response as AI-enabled attacks accelerate.

For enterprise AI leaders, this is a practical operating issue:
- identity and access controls need to assume more automated attack paths;
- detection and response must become faster and more contextual;
- AI model risk and cyber risk should be governed together.

The message is not to pause AI adoption. It is to make defensive readiness part of the AI roadmap from day one.
