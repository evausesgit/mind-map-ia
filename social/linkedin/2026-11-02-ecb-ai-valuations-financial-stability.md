AI valuations are now explicitly part of the financial-stability conversation.

The European Central Bank has identified high valuations in AI-linked equities, bond-market stress and potential losses in non-bank finance among the main current risks. These channels can reinforce one another through concentrated positioning, leverage, collateral pressure and liquidity mismatch.

For banks and market participants, the practical implications extend beyond directional exposure:
- map second-order concentration across indices, counterparties and collateral;
- stress correlated moves in AI equities, rates and credit;
- distinguish durable infrastructure demand from valuation momentum.

AI is increasingly both an operating capability and a market-risk factor. Governance needs to reflect both.
