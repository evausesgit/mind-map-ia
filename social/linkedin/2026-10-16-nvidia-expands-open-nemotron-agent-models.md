Open-weight agents are moving from a model contest to a deployment contest.

NVIDIA has expanded Nemotron with models for multimodal reasoning and more local agent workloads. The strategic move is clear: make the model layer fit naturally into NVIDIA’s inference, tooling and hardware stack.

Enterprise teams should test:
- quality on their own tools and documents;
- latency and cost after quantisation;
- licence, support and portability constraints.

An open checkpoint can reduce model dependency, but it does not automatically eliminate infrastructure dependency.
