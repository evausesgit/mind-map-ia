Frontier AI governance is becoming more concrete around cyber capability thresholds. OpenAI has outlined how it thinks about pacing model development as systems approach cyber-critical capabilities.

This matters because cyber risk is one of the first areas where model capability, enterprise exposure and public safety converge. The relevant controls are not just benchmark scores; they include staged deployment, access policy, monitoring and defensive preparedness.

For AI leaders, three questions stand out:
- Which model capabilities trigger additional cyber review?
- How should access change when a system can materially assist offensive workflows?
- What defensive readiness is required before broader rollout?

The direction of travel is clear: frontier model adoption will increasingly be tied to capability-based governance, not only vendor assurance.
