The next AI policy battle may be less about whether to regulate and more about who gets to set the rules.

Politico reports that Anthropic has circulated draft federal legislation defining when the US government could block the release of an advanced AI model, while pre-empting some state-level safety laws.

What matters for enterprises:
- Federal pre-emption could simplify compliance, but may also remove stricter local safeguards.
- Release thresholds would directly influence model availability and vendor roadmaps.
- A vendor-authored proposal is an input to the debate—not neutral policy and not enacted law.

AI leaders should track the final institutional design: testing standards, reporting duties, enforcement powers and the boundary between federal and state authority will shape procurement and model-risk obligations.
