Anthropic's public-market debut turns the frontier-model race into a much more measurable business story.

The shares opened at $111.50 after 980 million Class A shares were priced at $83, raising about $81.3 billion in what Reuters describes as the largest IPO on record.

For enterprise AI leaders, the important change is not the first-day price move. Public reporting should gradually make it easier to assess:

- revenue concentration and enterprise adoption;
- compute and infrastructure economics;
- the gap between technical momentum and sustainable margins.

A successful listing does not remove valuation or vendor-concentration risk. It gives boards, banks and customers a new set of market signals to monitor.
