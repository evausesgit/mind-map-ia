A large group of AI, technology, banking, and payments companies is warning that the cyber-risk clock is accelerating.

According to the BBC, 100 firms including Google, Microsoft, Anthropic, OpenAI, Capital One, Mastercard, and Visa signed an open letter urging stronger cyber defences before AI makes attacks more widespread and sophisticated within months.

This is especially relevant for regulated industries: AI security can no longer be separated from operational resilience.

What to watch:
- Defensive AI for critical infrastructure.
- Stronger testing and red-team requirements.
- Better monitoring for agentic systems with tool access.
