Enterprise reasoning models are starting to diverge from the general-purpose frontier race.

Salesforce and Nvidia have introduced Koa, built by post-training Nvidia’s open-weight Nemotron base on synthetic data for sales, marketing and customer-support tasks. Salesforce says no customer data was used, and Koa will sit alongside third-party models in Agentforce.

The design highlights three priorities that matter in production:
- task-specific quality rather than benchmark breadth;
- lower token consumption and more predictable economics;
- data controls and routing flexibility inside the enterprise platform.

For regulated firms, open weights do not remove the need for validation. But they can create useful options for deployment, inspection and cost control when paired with strong model-risk governance.
