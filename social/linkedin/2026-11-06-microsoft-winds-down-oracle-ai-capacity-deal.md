AI infrastructure strategy is shifting from securing capacity at almost any cost to selecting capacity more carefully.

Microsoft is reportedly winding down an arrangement to rent AI computing capacity from Oracle, within a broader reassessment of billions of dollars in cloud partnerships.

For enterprise buyers, three questions become more important:
- how concentrated is a provider's customer base?
- how portable are workloads if capacity plans change?
- what contractual exit and continuity mechanisms are actually tested?

Compute remains strategic, but resilience depends on more than headline GPU availability.
