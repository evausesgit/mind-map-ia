AI safety controls are becoming more contextual: the same capability can be restricted for general users and deliberately opened to vetted defenders.

Anthropic is expanding a programme that gives approved cybersecurity teams access to its most capable models with fewer restrictions. Reuters reports that Project Glasswing partners found at least 129,000 verified software vulnerabilities between April and July, with Anthropic’s own open-source scanning finding another 5,500 through October.

For regulated enterprises, this points to a useful governance pattern:
- access based on role, purpose and verification;
- enhanced monitoring for high-capability cyber use;
- measurable outcomes, with clear escalation and revocation paths.

The challenge is not choosing between capability and safeguards. It is designing controls that preserve legitimate defensive work without normalising unrestricted access.
