Enterprise agents become consequential when they can act inside systems of record.

ServiceNow and OpenAI are deepening their integration so ChatGPT and OpenAI agents can reach ServiceNow workflows through Action Fabric. The design emphasises enterprise context, permissions and approvals, with planned integrations spanning security operations, Codex and governance workflows.

That shifts the key questions from “Can the model answer?” to:
- Which actions can it initiate or complete?
- Which identity and approval chain applies?
- Can every decision and tool call be reconstructed?

For regulated firms, governed execution is the real product. Reasoning quality matters, but enforceable permissions, segregation of duties and rollback determine whether agents can move into production.
