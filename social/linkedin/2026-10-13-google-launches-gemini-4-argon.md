The frontier-model race is moving from chat quality to long-running, verifiable work.

Google has launched Gemini 4 Argon globally for developers and enterprises, positioning it for multi-step tasks in finance, legal, software engineering and cybersecurity. Introductory API pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with a 95% discount for cached input.

For enterprise AI leaders, the important questions are operational:
- Do the gains persist on internal, high-stakes workflows?
- Can outputs be traced, reproduced and challenged?
- Does caching materially improve unit economics without weakening data controls?

Vendor benchmarks are a useful starting point, not an adoption decision. The next step is a controlled evaluation against existing models, with quality, latency, cost and failure recovery measured together.
