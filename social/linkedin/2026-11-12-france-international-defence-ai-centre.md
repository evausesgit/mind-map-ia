Defence AI is becoming an institutional and sovereignty question, not only a technology programme.

France’s Armed Forces Ministry says it will create an international centre to promote and support the growing use of AI in defence. The announcement points toward shared operational practices, standards and cooperation at a time when Europe is seeking greater autonomy in critical technologies.

For enterprise AI leaders, defence is an extreme but useful governance case:
- assurance must survive real operational pressure;
- data lineage and model provenance matter;
- sovereignty and interoperability must be designed together.

The broader signal is that AI governance frameworks are increasingly sector-specific, operational and geopolitical.
