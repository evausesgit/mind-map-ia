The next phase of enterprise AI will not be won by using the largest model for every task.

Google’s Gemini 4 Hydrogen is designed for fast coding and agentic execution, with a one-million-token context window and API pricing of $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens.

Google’s most interesting claim is architectural: Antigravity’s Orchestrator dynamically routes work between models. On Terminal-Bench 4.0, it reportedly used 24% less quota and completed work 19% faster than a single-model approach.

For AI leaders, the lesson is practical:
- benchmark workflows, not only models;
- route planning and execution to different model tiers;
- measure quality, latency and total cost together.

Multi-model orchestration is becoming an operating-model capability, not just an engineering optimisation.
