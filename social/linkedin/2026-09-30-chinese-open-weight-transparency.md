Open weights do not automatically mean transparent governance.

Reuters Breakingviews argues that models from Alibaba, Z.AI and other Chinese labs are gaining traction abroad while their safety disclosures remain less detailed than those of leading US labs. For enterprises, access to weights solves only part of the control problem.

A serious adoption review still needs:
- reproducible capability and safety evaluations;
- clear data provenance and licence rights;
- vulnerability disclosure and patching processes;
- evidence that survives supplier and geopolitical change.

Open-weight models can improve portability and deployment control. Trust still has to be engineered and evidenced.
