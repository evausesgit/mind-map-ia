The next enterprise AI advantage may come from models that are narrower — and closer to the workflow.

Cadence has introduced domain models for semiconductor design that chipmakers can run in their own environments. That combination matters where product data and intellectual property cannot simply be sent to a general-purpose hosted service.

The strategic pattern extends beyond chip design:
- domain accuracy over broad benchmark leadership;
- deployment control near sensitive data;
- integration with the tools where decisions are made.

For regulated firms, the question is increasingly not “Which model is best?” but “Which governed model-and-workflow combination performs reliably under our constraints?”
