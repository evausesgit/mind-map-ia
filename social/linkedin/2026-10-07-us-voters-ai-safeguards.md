AI governance is becoming a mainstream political issue, not a specialist policy debate.

A Reuters/Ipsos survey found that 84% of registered US voters view AI as a threat to American workers. Bipartisan majorities support stricter regulation, while 56% back limits on data-center construction. The online survey covered 4,506 adults, including 3,526 registered voters, and has a two-point margin of error.

For enterprise AI leaders, the signal is broader than one poll:
- workforce impact now shapes trust in AI deployment;
- infrastructure choices are becoming local political and energy issues;
- voluntary principles may not satisfy public expectations for accountability.

Responsible adoption needs measurable worker-transition plans, credible controls and evidence of value—not only faster deployment. This survey is a snapshot, not a prediction, but it points to the questions regulators, employees and clients will increasingly ask.
