The European enterprise AI conversation is shifting from chat to execution.

OpenAI reports that Codex and ChatGPT Work now account for more than half of output tokens in its EU Enterprise and Business sample. Among EU businesses with 200 or fewer associated users, weekly active use of these tools has grown more than eightfold since May 2026.

Three cautions matter for AI leaders:
- vendor usage data is not a market-wide adoption survey;
- adoption varies widely across countries and organisations;
- deeper agent use increases the need for identity, permissions, monitoring and human escalation.

The strategic question is no longer only who has access to AI. It is which organisations can turn multi-step automation into measurable, controlled operating performance.
