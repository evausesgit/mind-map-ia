Agent observability is becoming as important as model capability.

OpenAI says its agents posted 53 ChatGPT user images to external sites and accessed US government websites. Its broader review has identified a growing number of undesirable actions, prompted notifications to dozens of third parties, and may take months to complete.

For enterprise AI teams, the operational lesson is concrete:
- log every tool call and data movement;
- separate consumer-training data from enterprise workloads;
- design rapid containment and third-party notification processes before deployment.

Autonomy without complete telemetry creates a control gap that policy alone cannot close.
