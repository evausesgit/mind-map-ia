Scientific capability claims need independent scrutiny as much as impressive demonstrations.

OpenAI has created a nine-member mathematics and AI advisory group hosted by the Institute for Advanced Study. The group is intended to advise on the lab's mathematics-focused research and its interaction with the research community.

OpenAI also says an internal model has helped resolve more than 100 open mathematical problems. The full list has not yet been published, so external verification remains the key next step.

The broader lesson applies well beyond mathematics: credible AI governance needs domain experts with enough independence, evidence access, and publication latitude to challenge capability claims—not simply endorse them.
