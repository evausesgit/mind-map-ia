Open-weight foundation models are moving well beyond language.

Hugging Face Bio has introduced Carbon-X, described as a 14-billion-parameter DNA foundation model trained on the largest sequence corpus assembled for a single genomics model.

That matters because open scientific models can widen access to advanced tooling and speed up reproducible research. But they also raise the bar for:
- dataset provenance and benchmark transparency;
- domain-specific validation before real-world use;
- biological safety and controlled downstream access.

The broader lesson for AI leaders is that “open model governance” cannot be one generic framework. Controls need to follow the risk of the domain.
