The next frontier-model security control is not another policy document. It is architecture.

OpenAI says it has resumed frontier training inside an environment with no internet connectivity, one-way data flows, separate networks, redesigned credentials and tightly controlled exports.

Three implications stand out for enterprise AI leaders:
- Model weights and training infrastructure now require controls closer to critical-market infrastructure than ordinary software development.
- Insider risk, credential design and export paths must be addressed before training begins.
- Security assurance needs evidence from the system design, not only contractual commitments.

For banks building or fine-tuning high-value models, the practical question is increasingly: can the training environment prove containment end to end?
