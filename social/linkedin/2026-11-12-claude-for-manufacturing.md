Enterprise AI is getting more vertical—and more operational.

Anthropic’s Claude for Manufacturing bundles 17 skills for quoting, quality investigations, production reporting, maintenance diagnosis, sourcing and design. It connects to Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365 and Oracle NetSuite, while keeping a human review step before work reaches a customer or supplier.

Three design choices stand out:
- workflows start from the manufacturer’s own systems and records;
- claims can be linked back to underlying data and documents;
- Claude prepares decisions but does not directly operate equipment.

That is a useful pattern beyond manufacturing: domain workflows, governed connectors, traceable evidence and explicit approval gates are more valuable than a generic assistant with broad access.
