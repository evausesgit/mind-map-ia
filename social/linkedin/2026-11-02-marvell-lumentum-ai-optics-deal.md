The AI infrastructure race is moving deeper into the network.

Marvell has agreed to acquire optical-components maker Lumentum in an all-stock transaction with an implied enterprise value of $115 billion including debt. The strategic thesis is that optical connectivity will become an increasingly important bottleneck as AI clusters grow in scale and bandwidth intensity.

The deal highlights three enterprise themes:
- AI capacity depends on networking and optics, not only accelerators;
- infrastructure consolidation can increase supply-chain and vendor concentration;
- large strategic bets still need disciplined integration and return assumptions.

For AI leaders, architecture choices should include interconnect capacity, resilience and total infrastructure economics—not just model and GPU availability.
