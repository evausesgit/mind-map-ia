Independent AI evaluation is moving inside the lab.

Anthropic and Accenture each expect to invest at least $1 billion over five years in an embedded-evaluation partnership led by Faculty. Evaluators are intended to receive employee-like access so they can observe model training, red-team systems, assess alignment and safeguards, and report incidents.

This could produce evidence that external, point-in-time testing cannot:
- how safety decisions are made during training;
- whether commitments survive delivery pressure;
- how incidents are escalated and disclosed.

Important details remain open, including access standards, reporting rights and funding independence. For regulated enterprises, the useful question is whether this model can generate assurance artefacts that customers and supervisors can actually rely on.
