AI supervision in banking is moving from broad principles to customer outcomes.

Australia’s corporate regulator is reviewing how banks use AI and what those systems mean for customers. That is an important shift: governance frameworks will increasingly be judged by their operational consequences, not by the quality of policy documents alone.

Banks should be ready to demonstrate:
- clear ownership for AI-supported decisions;
- testing for bias, errors, and customer harm;
- traceability, human escalation, and effective redress.

For regulated firms, the durable advantage will come from making AI controls observable and repeatable across the full model lifecycle.
