Anthropic’s Claude Duet makes the shift from “assistant on demand” to an always-available agent concrete.

The research preview can monitor connected apps, send a morning brief, and continue work in a cloud virtual machine after the user steps away. Anthropic says consequential actions still require approval, while securities trading and money transfers remain off-limits.

Three enterprise questions matter more than the demo:
- Can permissions be scoped to the task and revoked immediately?
- Are every tool call, data movement and approval independently auditable?
- How do background model turns affect cost and operational risk?

The product direction is compelling, but proactive agents need an identity, policy and evidence layer before they become trusted colleagues.
