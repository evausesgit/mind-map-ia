AI service outages are becoming a board-level operational resilience topic.

WIRED and Axios reported widespread disruption affecting major AI services including OpenAI and Anthropic on September 3, with limited public detail at the time of reporting. For organisations building copilots and agentic workflows, incidents like this test the maturity of the AI platform, not only the vendor status page.

Resilience patterns that matter:
- multi-model routing for critical use cases;
- graceful degradation when a model or API is unavailable;
- clear incident communications for business users and risk teams.

The lesson is simple: if AI is embedded in daily work, continuity planning must include AI dependencies explicitly.
