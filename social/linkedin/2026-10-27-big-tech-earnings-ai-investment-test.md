The next test for the AI boom is not another benchmark. It is the earnings statement.

Big Tech results will be read against sustained infrastructure spending and volatile markets. Investors will look for evidence that cloud demand, product adoption and utilisation are converting large AI capital programmes into durable revenue and margins.

The questions for enterprise AI leaders are closely related:
- which use cases are moving from experimentation to repeatable production value?
- are utilisation and unit economics improving as adoption grows?
- how much strategic exposure sits with a small number of model and infrastructure providers?

AI investment remains a long-duration operating-model decision. Clear usage economics, workload portability and benefit measurement are becoming as important as access to frontier capability.
