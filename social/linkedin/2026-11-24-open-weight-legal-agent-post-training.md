Open-weight model performance may depend less on the base-model leaderboard than on disciplined domain post-training.

Harvey and Baseten adapted four US open-weight models for legal, consulting, banking and automation tasks. Harvey reports that Meta’s Muse Spark Open improved from 19.2% to 30.3% all-pass on a Legal Agent Benchmark holdout, while three other US models passed 20%.

The enterprise takeaway is not that one model “wins.” It is that:
- domain training can materially change task performance;
- holdout design and all-pass criteria matter;
- self-reported results still require independent replication and internal evaluation.

Open weights create room to specialise, but evaluation governance remains essential.
