Open weights do not automatically mean low-cost deployment.

MiniMax has published M3.1 Flash on Hugging Face. The mixture-of-experts model has 428 billion parameters in total, activates 23 billion per token and supports a one-million-token context window; the repository is roughly 252 GB.

The release creates real optionality, but the enterprise trade-offs remain concrete:
- infrastructure and serving complexity;
- licence review for commercial use;
- security evaluation and artefact provenance.

Open-weight models can reduce data and vendor dependency when deployed well. Their value depends on the full operating envelope, not only the ability to download the files.
