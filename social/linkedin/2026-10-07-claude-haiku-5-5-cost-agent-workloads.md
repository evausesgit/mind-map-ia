The economics of agentic AI may be improving faster than many operating models.

Anthropic says Claude Haiku 5.5 costs about 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5 on average. For prompts up to 100,000 tokens, pricing starts at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens, targeting summarisation, classification, browser use and subagent workloads.

Lower unit cost can unlock high-volume workflows—but it also increases the importance of controls:
- route each task to the smallest model that passes internal evaluations;
- measure retries, tool calls and cache usage, not token price alone;
- keep approval and permission boundaries independent of model capability.

Cheap inference does not automatically mean cheap operations. Observability and workflow design determine the total cost.
