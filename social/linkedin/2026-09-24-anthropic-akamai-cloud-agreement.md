AI infrastructure commitments are becoming strategic balance-sheet decisions, not merely cloud contracts.

Anthropic has agreed to buy $11.6 billion of Akamai cloud services over seven years. Akamai also receives a warrant for up to a 5% stake, creating a direct link between capacity supply and Anthropic’s future value.

Three points stand out:
- model providers are diversifying beyond the largest hyperscalers;
- long-duration compute commitments are creating concentration and execution risk;
- equity-linked infrastructure deals complicate vendor and counterparty assessment.

For enterprise AI leaders, model quality is only one dependency. Capacity resilience, contractual portability and provider financial exposure now belong in the same governance conversation.
