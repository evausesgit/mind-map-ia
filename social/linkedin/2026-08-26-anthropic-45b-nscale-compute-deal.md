Frontier AI is increasingly a compute-finance story. Anthropic's reported $45B deal with Nscale shows how model competition is being shaped by data-center capacity, power access, and long-term infrastructure commitments.

For enterprises, this matters because the reliability and pricing of AI services are linked to supply chains that sit far beyond the model API: GPUs, energy, data centers, networking, and financing structures.

Three questions are worth asking in any AI roadmap:
- Which use cases truly require frontier-scale models?
- What happens if capacity or pricing changes?
- How much should be diversified across providers, open-weight models, and internal controls?
