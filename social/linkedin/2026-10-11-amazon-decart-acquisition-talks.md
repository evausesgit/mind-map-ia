The AI capability race may be shifting from partnerships to acquisition.

Amazon is reportedly in talks to acquire generative-video startup Decart in a deal valued at around $7 billion. The transaction is not confirmed, but the reported discussions show how strategically valuable real-time generative media and model talent have become to cloud platforms.

For enterprise buyers, consolidation can have two opposite effects:
- faster integration of specialist capabilities into major cloud stacks;
- greater dependency on a small number of vendors and commercial roadmaps.

AI architecture decisions should therefore include portability, exit options and contractual continuity—not only model quality.
