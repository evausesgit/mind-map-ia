Multimodal retrieval is becoming a practical open-model building block.

Google has published the technical report for EmbeddingGemma 2, an open embedding model designed to place text, images, audio, video and mixed inputs in one vector space. The compact architecture builds on Gemma 4 and Gemini Embedding 2.

This matters beyond benchmarks. A unified embedding layer can support search and RAG across research notes, charts, calls, presentations and video without sending every asset through a large hosted model.

Before enterprise adoption, teams should still test:
- retrieval quality on domain-specific and multilingual content;
- licence and deployment constraints;
- privacy, indexing security and vector-store access controls.

Open-weight infrastructure can improve control and reduce dependency — but only when evaluation and governance travel with the model.
