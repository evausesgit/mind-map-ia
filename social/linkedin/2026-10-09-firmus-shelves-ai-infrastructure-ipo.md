AI infrastructure demand may be strong, but capital markets are becoming more selective about the price paid for that growth.

Australian data-centre operator Firmus has withdrawn a planned $5 billion IPO amid market volatility and closer scrutiny of AI infrastructure valuations. The proposed offering reportedly targeted a $30.6 billion equity valuation, versus $10.5 billion in an August funding round.

The signal for enterprise and investment leaders is not that AI infrastructure demand has disappeared. It is that investors increasingly want a credible bridge from contracted capacity to utilisation, cash generation and sustainable returns.

Watch the fundamentals behind the narrative:
- customer and power concentration;
- financing costs and build-out timing;
- contracted demand versus realised utilisation.
