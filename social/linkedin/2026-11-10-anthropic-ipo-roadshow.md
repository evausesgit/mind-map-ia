Frontier AI is moving into a new phase of public-market scrutiny.

Anthropic has launched its IPO roadshow with 825 million Class A shares and a proposed range of $70 to $78. The company has applied to list on Nasdaq as ANT, with media calculations putting the implied valuation near $1.4 trillion at the top of the range.

Public investors will now test assumptions that private markets could defer: compute intensity, gross-margin trajectory, customer concentration, infrastructure commitments and the durability of model differentiation.

For enterprise buyers, financial transparency can improve vendor assessment — but it can also increase pressure on providers to optimise pricing, utilisation and product lock-in.
