Open-weight models are becoming an enterprise control question, not only a model-choice question.

Palo Alto Networks has agreed to acquire Applied Compute, whose tooling supports training and deployment of open-weight models. The strategic direction is notable: combine model control with cybersecurity and operational governance.

Buyers should test supported licences and deployment patterns, model lineage and runtime controls, and whether integration reduces risk without creating a new platform dependency.

“Open-weight” and “secure” are not interchangeable labels. Assurance must be measurable.
