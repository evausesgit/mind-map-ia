Model distribution is now part of the enterprise AI supply chain.

OpenAI’s post on the Hugging Face incident frames a broader issue: model hubs, weights, adapters, datasets and evaluation artefacts are now critical dependencies.

For AI leaders, this means supply-chain discipline has to extend into the model layer:
- provenance and integrity checks;
- trusted registries and approval gates;
- incident playbooks for compromised or suspicious model assets.

The lesson is simple: open ecosystems are powerful, but production adoption requires controls that look much closer to software supply-chain security.
