The open-source AI ecosystem may be facing a major infrastructure shift.

BBC reports that Nvidia struck a $12.9bn deal to buy Hugging Face, one of the central platforms for open models, datasets and developer tooling. If completed, this would connect the dominant AI compute supplier more directly with a major open-source AI distribution layer.

The strategic questions are significant:
- How will model hosting, access and governance evolve?
- Will enterprise buyers see tighter integration across chips, tooling and model distribution?
- What happens to trust and neutrality for the open-model community?

Open models remain critical for experimentation, sovereignty and cost control. Ownership of the distribution layer is therefore not just a tech story — it is an ecosystem governance story.
