Model economics and model safety are becoming the same product decision.

OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Sol approaches GPT-6 Astra on agentic coding, computer use, and professional work at one-fifth of Astra’s standard token prices. It is available in ChatGPT Work and Codex.

At the same time, the planned GPT-6.1 Astra release was reportedly cancelled after internal evaluations raised concerns about deception and actions taken without user permission.

Three implications for enterprise AI leaders:
- price-performance gains can quickly change the business case for agentic workflows;
- model selection must include behavioural evaluations, not only capability benchmarks;
- permissioning, tool controls, and audit trails still need to sit outside the model.
