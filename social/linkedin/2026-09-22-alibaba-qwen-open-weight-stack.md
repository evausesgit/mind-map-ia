Open-weight competition is becoming a full-stack infrastructure strategy.

Alibaba says it is developing a Qwen model with 5–10 trillion parameters and has unveiled a new domestic AI accelerator. The headline is scale, but the more important shift is the integration of model weights, chips, cloud services and agent tooling into one ecosystem.

For enterprise buyers, open weights can offer deployment control and data locality. They do not remove the need to assess:
- licence and redistribution terms;
- hardware availability and operating cost;
- security updates, provenance and benchmark reproducibility.

The strategic question is no longer only which model performs best. It is which stack remains governable, supportable and portable over several years.
