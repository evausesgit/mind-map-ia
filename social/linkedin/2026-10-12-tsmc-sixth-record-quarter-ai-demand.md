AI infrastructure spending is still showing up in semiconductor earnings—but expectations are becoming harder to beat.

Analysts expect TSMC to report a sixth consecutive record quarterly profit, with net income near T$746 billion, roughly 65% higher year on year. Thursday's results should provide a useful read on advanced-node demand and capacity discipline.

For enterprise AI leaders, the implications extend beyond chip equities:
- sustained demand can keep compute capacity and pricing under pressure;
- concentration around a small number of advanced manufacturers remains a resilience risk;
- supplier guidance matters as much as benchmark progress when planning AI capacity.

The next question is whether demand remains broad and contracted, or increasingly dependent on a narrow set of hyperscalers.
