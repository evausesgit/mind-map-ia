The frontier-model race is also becoming a capital-markets race.

Reuters reports that OpenAI is seeking investor commitments on an accelerated timetable ahead of Anthropic’s expected mid-November IPO. The potential valuation cited is above $1.4 trillion, although the terms and timing are not final.

This matters beyond the headline valuation:
- the largest labs increasingly compete for the same capital;
- infrastructure commitments raise the cost of staying at the frontier;
- public-market scrutiny could expose more economics, concentration and governance data.

For enterprise buyers, financial durability and funding dependency now belong in strategic vendor assessment alongside model quality and security.
