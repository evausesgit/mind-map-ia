Sovereign AI is becoming industrial policy at frontier-model scale.

South Korea plans to launch a 4.7 trillion-won ($3.5 billion) programme in March 2027 to develop a frontier AI model through competitive tenders. The initiative reflects a broader concern: strategic AI capability depends on compute, data, talent and deployment infrastructure—not only access to an API.

For enterprises, national investment programmes can reshape:
- the range of local and regional model suppliers;
- data-residency and procurement options;
- access to compute and specialised talent;
- geopolitical and concentration risk.

The key test will be whether public investment creates reusable capability and a competitive ecosystem, rather than a single expensive model.
