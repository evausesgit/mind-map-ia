California is turning AI risk assessment into a pre-release obligation for chatbot operators.

A new child-safety package requires risk assessments before AI chatbots are rolled out and restricts addictive social feeds for users under 16. Separate laws signed a day earlier establish standards for independent frontier-model safety evaluations and a registry of financially independent AI auditors.

The policy signal extends beyond consumer technology:

- risk evidence is expected before deployment, not after harm occurs;
- evaluator independence is becoming a formal governance concern;
- product controls increasingly need to account for vulnerable user groups.

Organizations should expect model assurance to move closer to established audit disciplines, with documented testing, independence, and accountable release decisions.
