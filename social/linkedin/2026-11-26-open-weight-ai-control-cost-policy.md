Open-weight AI is no longer only a model-selection question. It is becoming an enterprise architecture, security and industrial-policy decision.

The attraction is clear: private deployment, customization, cost control and reduced vendor lock-in. The hard part is equally clear: released weights cannot be recalled, safety controls can be modified, and internal teams inherit more responsibility for evaluation, patching and monitoring.

A practical enterprise decision should compare:
- control and data residency;
- total operating cost, not just inference price;
- model provenance, licence and security lifecycle.

The strongest strategy may be a governed portfolio rather than an ideological choice between open and closed models.
