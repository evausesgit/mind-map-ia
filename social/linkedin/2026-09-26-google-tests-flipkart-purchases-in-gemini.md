Generative search is moving from recommendation to transaction.

Google is testing a Buy button for selected Flipkart products inside Gemini and AI Mode in India. The limited trial opens a Flipkart-branded checkout without requiring users to leave the AI interface, and a broader rollout may follow in October.

This matters beyond retail:

- the assistant becomes part of the execution layer, not just the discovery layer;
- attribution, consent and transaction liability become product requirements;
- platform ranking can directly influence economic outcomes.

For regulated sectors, this is a useful preview of the controls required when conversational systems progress from answering to acting.
