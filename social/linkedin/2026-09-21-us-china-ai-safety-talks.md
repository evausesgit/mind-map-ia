AI incident management is starting to become a channel of strategic diplomacy.

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent says senior American and Chinese officials will meet in Shenzhen in about two months to discuss AI dangers and communication protocols for safety incidents.

The announcement matters because crisis communication can reduce escalation risk, but the operational details are still missing:
- what qualifies as a reportable incident;
- how quickly each side must notify the other;
- how claims can be verified without exposing sensitive systems.

Global firms should not wait for interstate rules to mature. Clear internal incident thresholds, escalation paths, and cross-border notification playbooks are already necessary for high-impact AI systems.
