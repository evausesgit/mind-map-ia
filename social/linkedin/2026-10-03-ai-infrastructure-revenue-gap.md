The AI infrastructure debate is becoming a monetisation debate.

A Reuters analysis estimates that hyperscalers and other participants may need more than $4.2 trillion in new revenue to justify the current build-out. At the same time, economy-wide productivity gains remain difficult to measure.

That does not prove an AI bubble. It does change the questions leaders and investors should ask:
- Which workloads create measurable cash flow or cost reduction?
- Who carries utilisation and refinancing risk?
- How quickly do chips and data-centre assets depreciate economically?

Capability matters. So do unit economics, adoption and durable demand.
