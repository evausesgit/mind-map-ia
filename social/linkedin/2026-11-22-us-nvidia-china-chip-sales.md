AI infrastructure strategy is increasingly inseparable from export controls and geopolitical risk.

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent says Washington is seeking answers from Nvidia about chip sales to China and wants clarity on compliance responsibility. The inquiry does not establish wrongdoing, but it adds another layer of uncertainty around the dominant supplier of AI accelerators.

Enterprise implications include:
- potential constraints on hardware availability and regional deployment;
- tighter vendor and supply-chain due diligence;
- greater exposure to sudden policy, licensing and compliance changes.

AI capacity planning should therefore include geopolitical concentration and regulatory change as first-class risks—not treat compute as a purely technical procurement decision.
