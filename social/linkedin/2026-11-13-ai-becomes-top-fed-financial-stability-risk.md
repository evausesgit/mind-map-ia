AI has moved from a technology agenda item to a financial-stability concern.

In the Federal Reserve’s latest survey, 87% of market contacts identified AI as a salient risk to the financial system. The concern is broader than model safety: it includes crowded exposures, high valuations, cyber risk and the chance of abrupt repricing if investment expectations weaken.

For financial institutions, this reinforces three priorities:
- map direct and indirect AI concentration risk;
- connect technology scenarios with market and operational stress tests;
- treat third-party compute and model dependencies as financial resilience issues.

AI governance now needs a line of sight into balance-sheet, market and systemic risk—not only compliance.
