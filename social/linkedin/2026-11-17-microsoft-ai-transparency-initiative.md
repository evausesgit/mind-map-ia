Enterprise agents need evidence trails, not just model cards.

Microsoft has launched an AI Transparency Initiative with more than 40 organizations, including Accenture, Adobe, KPMG, Mastercard and Volvo Cars. The stated goal is a common approach to transparency, security and governance across models and providers.

The important point is what this is — and what it is not. It is a standards-development effort, not yet a completed assurance system.

For AI leaders, useful transparency should eventually make it possible to reconstruct:
- which model, policy and data were used;
- what tools an agent called and with which permissions;
- what outcome occurred, who approved it and how it can be audited.

Cross-provider interoperability will matter if organizations want governance without locking controls to a single platform.
