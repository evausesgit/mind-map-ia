AI infrastructure strategy is increasingly an energy strategy.

Anthropic has reportedly agreed to a $3.4 billion order covering 1.7 GW of on-site generation for AI data centers. Beyond the headline number, this points to a structural constraint: model roadmaps now depend on power procurement, permitting, grid connections and operational resilience.

For enterprise AI leaders, three implications stand out:
- provider resilience should include energy and capacity dependencies;
- long-term model economics depend on infrastructure execution, not only token pricing;
- concentration risk grows when frontier capability and scarce compute remain tightly coupled.

The next phase of AI competition may be decided as much by megawatts and delivery schedules as by benchmarks.
