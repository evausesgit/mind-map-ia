Enterprise AI is moving from standalone assistants into the systems where core work actually happens.

OpenAI and Oracle are extending their partnership with Oracle AI Workspace, designed to connect Oracle applications and governed enterprise data to ChatGPT. OpenAI says Oracle already has 130,000 active ChatGPT users and more than 95,000 active Codex users.

Three points stand out:
- access is intended to follow existing organisational permissions and approvals;
- finance, procurement and planning workflows can be brought into one conversational environment;
- security and administration will matter as much as model capability.

For enterprise AI leaders, this is another sign that the strategic battleground is shifting toward governed workflow integration. The opportunity is substantial, but so are the questions around entitlements, auditability, segregation of duties and dependency on a tightly coupled vendor stack.
