Open-weight AI is expanding from language and vision into physical action.

Runway has previewed Praxis-1, a world-action model that uses large-scale video pretraining for robotic control. It is being tested with Noble Machines, Standard Bots and Ultra, with public release planned in the coming months.

Runway reports a 0.95 correlation between world-model simulations and real-world policy outcomes. That is promising, but independent replication, safety evidence and final licence terms will matter before enterprise adoption.

For builders, open weights could improve hardware interoperability and local control. For governance teams, embodied models also introduce a higher consequence of failure: actions affect physical environments, not only information flows.
