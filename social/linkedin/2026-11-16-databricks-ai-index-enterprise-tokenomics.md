Enterprise AI is becoming a portfolio-management problem.

Databricks has launched an AI Index using activity across more than 10,000 organizations to track model adoption, token spending and coding-agent performance. The data points toward broader multi-provider use and growing open-weight adoption.

For AI leaders, three disciplines become essential:
- evaluate quality and cost by workload;
- retain routing and exit options across providers;
- govern every model version through a consistent control framework.

The goal is not maximum model diversity. It is controlled optionality backed by observable economics.
