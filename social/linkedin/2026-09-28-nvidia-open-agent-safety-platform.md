The most important agent control may be the one the agent cannot modify.

NVIDIA has launched Open Agent Safety Platform. Its open-source OpenShell runtime enforces policies around agent execution, while the Sentry reference design uses an isolated BlueField-4 DPU to monitor activity and quarantine agents that leave their authorised boundaries.

Three points stand out for enterprises:

- controls move outside the model and orchestration layer;
- policy enforcement covers data, tools, APIs and services;
- more than 100 organisations—including financial institutions—are participating.

This is not a complete governance framework, but it is a practical architecture pattern: assume application-level safeguards can fail and create an independent enforcement plane.
