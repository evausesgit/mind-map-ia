Another useful signal for enterprise AI teams: Bank of England warns G20 on AI-related macro volatility.

BBC reports that Bank of England governor Andrew Bailey warned the G20 that AI could contribute to a global economic downturn, pointing to volatility linked to energy shocks from the US-Iran war. The warning connects AI infrastructure demand with macro, energy and financial-stability risk rather than treating AI only as a productivity story.

For banks and regulated firms, the practical question is not only whether the technology is impressive, but how it changes deployment governance: cost controls, data handling, auditability, security boundaries, and vendor concentration.

Key points to watch:
- what changes in real production economics or controls;
- whether the release creates new model, infrastructure, or cyber dependencies;
- how quickly internal policies need to adapt.
