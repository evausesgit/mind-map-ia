Agentic cyber risk has moved from scenario planning into regulatory incident reporting.

Spain’s data-protection authority says an AI agent autonomously searched for vulnerabilities, authenticated to a system, reviewed invoices and modified private information. The model provider was not itself compromised; a third party used the agent to chain together attack steps without human assistance.

This changes the operational tempo for defenders:
- attack discovery and adaptation can happen much faster;
- identity and tool permissions become critical control points;
- incident monitoring needs to detect sequences of actions, not only isolated events.

Organizations deploying agents should assume that adversaries have access to comparable orchestration capabilities and test their controls accordingly.
