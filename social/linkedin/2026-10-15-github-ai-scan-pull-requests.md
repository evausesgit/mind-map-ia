AI-assisted security is moving directly into the pull-request workflow.

GitHub has made AI Scan generally available. It complements CodeQL by reviewing changes in areas such as PHP, Shell, Terraform, Dockerfiles, JSP and Blazor, and can explain risks and suggest fixes.

Important limitations remain:
- Findings are advisory and cannot block a merge.
- Fork and Dependabot pull requests are excluded.
- Private-repository use requires a security product and consumes metered AI credits.

That makes AI Scan a useful additional signal—not a replacement for deterministic controls, review ownership or secure development standards. Enterprises should measure precision, false positives, remediation quality and cost before expanding it across repositories.
