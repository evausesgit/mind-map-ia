AI infrastructure is moving closer to the core market-data stack.

LSEG has partnered with Nvidia to help financial institutions process and analyse large volumes of historical market data faster and at scale. The direction is strategically important for research, backtesting and data-intensive analytics, where compute placement and data access can become the real bottlenecks.

Before production adoption, firms will still need evidence on:
- latency and reproducibility across workloads;
- data entitlements, lineage and access controls;
- full cost, resilience and vendor concentration.

For capital-markets AI leaders, this is a reminder that competitive advantage will not come from the model layer alone. Data architecture, accelerated compute and governance are converging into one operating stack.
