Sovereign AI is increasingly an infrastructure program, not just a model choice.

NVIDIA and eight Australian cloud and data-center partners are targeting up to 2 GW of AI-factory capacity by 2027. Sharon AI alone plans to deploy as many as 68,000 NVIDIA GPUs, while the wider program connects local compute with NVIDIA’s Nemotron open models.

For enterprise AI leaders, this illustrates three dependencies:
- local capacity and data-residency requirements;
- long-duration access to power, cooling, networking, and accelerators;
- software and model portability within a concentrated hardware ecosystem.

Regional compute can improve resilience and sovereignty, but it still requires disciplined concentration-risk and economics analysis.
