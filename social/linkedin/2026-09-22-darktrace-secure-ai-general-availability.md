Enterprise AI governance is shifting from inventories and policy documents toward runtime observation.

Darktrace has made SECURE AI generally available. The product is designed to discover shadow AI, scan sessions for jailbreak attempts, sensitive-data exposure and indirect prompt injection, and map AI agents to the people and permissions behind them.

Its telemetry illustrates the control problem:
- More than 80% of customer accounts across roughly 8,200 deployments used generative AI services in August.
- The average organisation used five AI providers.
- One early deployment found nearly 90 agents built in a low-code environment without an approval process.

The broader lesson is vendor-independent: agent governance needs identity, permission, data-flow and behavioural telemetry linked in one control plane. A model registry alone is no longer enough.
