AI infrastructure concentration is also leadership concentration.

Following Jensen Huang’s ousting, Elon Musk argued that Nvidia could lose critical deals. His statement is an opinion, not an established outcome, but it highlights how closely customer trust, supplier relationships and execution have been associated with one executive.

For enterprise buyers, the practical questions are immediate:
- Is the accelerator roadmap still credible?
- Are capacity and support commitments resilient to leadership change?
- Where can architecture reduce single-vendor dependency?

The lesson is broader than Nvidia: strategic AI programmes need succession and concentration scenarios in their vendor-risk framework.
