A new frontier-model launch is no longer just a product milestone. It is also an operational risk event for every enterprise planning to build agents on top of external AI platforms.

OpenAI has announced GPT-6 Astra, while Reuters and CNBC coverage highlighted growing scrutiny of advanced cyber capabilities and agent safety. The practical question for enterprises is not only whether the model is more capable, but whether controls, monitoring and fallback paths are mature enough for production workflows.

Key areas to watch:
- cyber-use evaluations and policy boundaries;
- agent authorization, tool-use and audit trails;
- dependency risk when one model provider becomes embedded in core processes.

For banks and regulated firms, frontier models are becoming infrastructure. That means model selection needs to sit alongside resilience, third-party risk and security governance — not outside it.
