The open-weight frontier is shifting from parameter scale alone toward inference efficiency and agentic reinforcement learning.

Reflection has introduced Beam, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 501 billion total parameters and 23 billion active. The company says it pretrained Beam on 23.8 trillion tokens and generated more than 100 million RL rollouts using 10,500 Nvidia GB300 GPUs over four weeks.

Reflection plans to release the weights, technical report and model card later in October. Until then, its benchmark and efficiency claims should be treated as vendor-reported and validated independently.

For enterprise teams, the interesting question is whether lower active-parameter compute can translate into a better capability-cost-control trade-off for coding and agent workflows.
