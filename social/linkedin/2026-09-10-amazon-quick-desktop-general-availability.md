Enterprise assistants are becoming an operating layer for everyday work, not just a chat interface.

AWS has made Amazon Quick generally available on macOS and Windows. Its mobile activity feed consolidates signals from email, calendars, CRM, and messaging while agents handle routine items in the background.

AWS highlights several enterprise controls:

- audit trails through Amazon CloudWatch and AWS CloudTrail;
- support for HIPAA, FedRAMP, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 requirements;
- shared workspaces for dashboards, agents, and automations.

For large organizations, the real adoption question is whether autonomy, permissions, evidence, and human approval are designed together. Productivity gains are useful only when actions remain observable and reversible.
