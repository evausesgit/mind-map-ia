The enterprise AI conversation is shifting from “Can we build an agent?” to “Can we operate thousands of agents safely?”

At Ignite 2026, Microsoft introduced a production stack that combines policy enforcement, isolated execution, agent identities and lifecycle governance. Agent Grid, Agent Sandbox for AKS, Entra Agent ID and Agent 365 are designed to address the control plane around autonomous systems.

The practical priorities are becoming clearer:
- a unique identity and accountable owner for every agent;
- enforceable policies at tool and agent boundaries;
- auditable lifecycle management across cloud and hybrid environments.

For AI leaders, platform selection should now be evaluated as much on governance portability and operational controls as on model quality.
