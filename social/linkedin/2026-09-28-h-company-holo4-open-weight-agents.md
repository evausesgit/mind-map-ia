Open-weight models are moving from chat into computer-use agents.

H Company’s Holo4 family combines GUI operation, code execution, MCP and API use in two checkpoints: a 27B dense model under a non-commercial licence and a 35B-A3B mixture-of-experts model under Apache 2.0. The company reports strong OSWorld performance and low per-task cost, although independent replication remains essential.

For enterprise teams, the release creates a useful evaluation option:

- local deployment can improve control and observability;
- the Apache-licensed checkpoint offers a clearer commercial path;
- benchmark success must still be tested on long workflows, internal applications and real approval policies.

Open weights reduce vendor dependency, but they do not remove model risk or operating responsibility.
