AI safety is becoming an operational control problem.

Reuters covered research warning that leading AI firms still cannot reliably contain what they have built, particularly as models become more agentic and more connected to tools.

For builders and enterprise adopters, the implication is concrete:
- containment and monitoring need to be designed before deployment;
- red teaming should include tool-use and escalation scenarios;
- incident response must account for autonomous or semi-autonomous actions.

This is especially relevant in regulated environments where auditability, kill switches, and human approval paths are not optional.
