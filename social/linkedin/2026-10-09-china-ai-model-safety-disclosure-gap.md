Open-weight adoption is accelerating faster than safety disclosure is standardising.

According to Reuters, SemiAnalysis reviewed 857 Chinese AI model releases from 2021 onward and found model-specific safety-test results published for only 3.6% of them.

This matters because strong benchmark scores and downloadable weights do not answer the questions enterprise risk teams need to resolve:
- What adversarial and misuse testing was performed?
- Which limitations were observed, and under what deployment conditions?
- Can the results be independently reproduced?

For regulated organisations, model provenance, evaluation evidence and deployment controls should be assessed separately from licence availability and headline performance.
