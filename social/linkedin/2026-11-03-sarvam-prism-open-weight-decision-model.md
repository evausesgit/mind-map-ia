Enterprise AI does not always need another long-form reasoning model. Sometimes it needs a fast, calibrated decision.

Sarvam Prism is an open-weight model for high-volume classification and routing. Sarvam reports a 71.8 average and 44 ms median latency on a B200, but these vendor-run benchmarks should be independently reproduced.

- Open weights support private deployment and domain adaptation
- Calibrated confidence can enable threshold-based human review
- Latency and accuracy must be retested on each bank workflow

For AI leaders, the practical question is how this changes architecture, governance, resilience and cost decisions in production.
