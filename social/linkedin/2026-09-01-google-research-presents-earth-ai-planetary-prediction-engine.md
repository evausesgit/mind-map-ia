Foundation models are moving into operational risk domains.

Google Research published work on a planetary prediction engine that automates global models through Earth AI. The focus is environmental and geophysical forecasting, but the broader pattern matters: AI is being used to orchestrate complex simulations and scenario systems, not only generate text.

For enterprise leaders, this raises practical questions:
- how to validate AI-assisted forecasts;
- how to explain model-driven scenarios to decision committees;
- how to connect uncertainty estimates to risk appetite.

For financial services, the relevance is clear: climate, supply-chain, insurance and macro-risk workflows increasingly depend on trustworthy AI-enabled modelling.
