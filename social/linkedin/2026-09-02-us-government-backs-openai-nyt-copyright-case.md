The copyright boundary for generative AI remains one of the biggest unresolved enterprise risks.

The Financial Times reports that the US government has backed OpenAI in its dispute with The New York Times, a case closely watched for its implications on training data, fair use, discovery and liability.

Why this matters for AI leaders:
- procurement and model-risk reviews still need explicit IP and data-provenance questions;
- legal uncertainty can affect vendor selection and indemnity discussions;
- policy outcomes may diverge across the US, EU and UK.

For enterprises, the practical stance should be neither panic nor complacency: document use cases, separate training from retrieval, and keep contractual protections visible.
