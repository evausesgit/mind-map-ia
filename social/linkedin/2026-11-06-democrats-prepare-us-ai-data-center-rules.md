AI policy risk in the US has moved from campaign signal to legislative agenda.

Reuters reports that Democrats plan a broad wave of bills on AI systems and data-center development after taking control of Congress. The details still need to pass through the legislative process, but the direction matters now.

Enterprise AI leaders should prepare for scrutiny across three connected layers:
- model accountability and safety;
- data-center energy and community impacts;
- evidence that governance works in production, not only on paper.

For regulated firms, this is another reason to keep model inventories, control evidence and infrastructure dependencies ready for change.
