The next phase of enterprise AI may be won by routing, not by always choosing the largest model.

SAP is replacing expensive models with cheaper alternatives where performance remains sufficient. It expects the move to contribute to more than €2 billion in efficiency gains over two years, even while its overall AI spending continues to rise.

This is a practical operating-model lesson:
- define quality thresholds by task;
- route low-risk work to the cheapest model that meets them;
- monitor drift, exceptions and unit cost continuously.

Model choice is becoming a portfolio decision. The organisations that can switch providers and models without rebuilding every workflow will have a structural cost and resilience advantage.
