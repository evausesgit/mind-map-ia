Open AI models are moving beyond language into physical-risk modelling.

Ai2 has released HiRO-ACEv2 through Hugging Face. The workflow downscales global climate simulations to around 3 km resolution, aiming to represent hurricanes, intense rainfall and mountain winds at a fraction of the cost of traditional storm-resolving simulation.

Ai2 estimates that global 3 km data can be generated at roughly 1/300th of the cost of X-SHiELD. That could broaden access to scenario generation, but it does not remove the need for validation against observed events, local datasets and model uncertainty.

For financial institutions, the opportunity is relevant to insurance, collateral, supply-chain and portfolio climate risk. The governance challenge is to prevent a cheaper scenario engine from being mistaken for a validated risk model.
