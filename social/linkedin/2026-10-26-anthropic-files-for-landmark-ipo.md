Anthropic’s IPO filing turns the economics of frontier AI into a public-market question.

The prospectus reports $16.3 billion in revenue alongside an $80.7 billion net loss. Whatever the accounting drivers behind those headline figures, investors now have a much clearer basis for examining the cost structure of model development and deployment.

Three questions stand out:
- How durable is customer growth once inference prices keep falling?
- How concentrated are compute, cloud and distribution dependencies?
- What does a credible route to sustainable margins look like?

For enterprise AI leaders, this is more than an IPO story. Supplier resilience, long-term pricing and portability should be part of vendor governance — not assumptions left outside the business case.
