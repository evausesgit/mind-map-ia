Open-weight models are moving closer to production-grade enterprise agents — and Tencent's Hy4 is a useful signal of where that competition is heading.

Hy4 combines multimodal reasoning, tool calling and computer use. Tencent says model weights and system cards will follow in the coming days, with explicit attention to cybersecurity and biosafety.

Three details stand out for enterprise teams:
- a blind internal financial evaluation covered 579 real-world tasks and 213 professionals;
- Tencent reports a 3.04 score for Hy4, versus 2.97 for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017;
- API pricing starts at $0.90 per million input tokens and $2.70 per million output tokens.

The benchmark is vendor-reported and still needs independent replication. But the combination of downloadable weights, finance-oriented evaluation and low API pricing increases the strategic value of testing open-weight alternatives — especially where deployment control, model portability and data residency matter.
