Open-weight AI creates valuable sovereignty and customisation options — and a different control problem.

RUSI notes that weights cannot be recalled or centrally patched once distributed. It also cites research that removed Llama 3 8B’s safety training in roughly five minutes on one GPU for under one dollar, illustrating how fragile model-level refusals can be.

The policy challenge is therefore broader than model licensing:
- investigators need clarity on powers and jurisdiction;
- inference and infrastructure providers need workable escalation channels;
- enterprises need deployment-layer controls that do not depend on the original model safeguards.

The right response is not to treat all open-weight use as unsafe, but to govern capability, deployment context and access separately.
