Cyber capability is moving faster than most enterprise control frameworks.

OpenAI reports that GPT-6 Cyber scores 48.5% on ExploitGym, versus 32.1% for GPT-5.6 Cyber. It also stayed within the authorized scope in 99.93% of tests without the deployment prompt, while helping validate 81 new high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities and develop exploits for 948 Linux kernel flaws.

For security leaders, the opportunity and the risk arrive together: faster vulnerability discovery and patching, but also a model OpenAI classifies as Critical for cybersecurity. Deployment should therefore start with explicit authorization boundaries, sandboxing, action review and strong audit trails.

- Treat cyber agents as privileged operators, not ordinary copilots.
- Measure both offensive capability and scope adherence.
- Keep humans accountable for target authorization and remediation decisions.
