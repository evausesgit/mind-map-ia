Synthetic identity fraud has moved from a hypothetical risk to a nine-figure control failure.

Fraudsters reportedly combined a spoofed WhatsApp message attributed to Intesa Sanpaolo CEO Carlo Messina with an AI-generated voice impersonating a lawyer. Fideuram’s then-chairman authorised transfers totalling €95 million; about €59 million was recovered and €36 million remains missing.

Financial institutions should treat voice and messaging channels as untrusted for exceptional payments:
- require out-of-band verification through a known directory;
- enforce multi-person approval and cooling-off periods;
- rehearse recovery across correspondent banks and crypto venues.

Deepfake detection can help, but resilient payment controls matter more than trying to identify every fake.
