Open-weight AI is moving from an engineering preference to a board-level cost strategy.

A Capgemini study reported by Reuters found that nearly 60% of senior executives at large companies plan to increase their use of lower-cost open-weight models over the next year. Respondents said AI spending was exceeding budgets by 24% on average.

That does not make open-weight deployment automatically cheaper or safer. Infrastructure, evaluation, security hardening and operations still have to be funded. But it changes the sourcing conversation:
- portability becomes leverage;
- self-hosting can support data-control requirements;
- workload-level model selection can reduce vendor concentration and inference costs.

The right comparison is not licence price alone. It is risk-adjusted total cost per successful business outcome.
