AI-assisted cyber defence has moved from experiments to infrastructure-scale operations.

OpenAI says NATO’s Communications and Information Agency has used up to 300 billion raw input and output tokens per month for software review, suspicious-activity investigation and vulnerability validation. A separate ENISA and CERT-EU review found that maintainers confirmed 95% of 88 AI-flagged potential issues as genuine.

For regulated enterprises, the lesson is not simply “deploy a cyber agent.” It is to build an operating model around it:
- keep humans accountable for validation and remediation;
- log model usage, findings, false positives and downstream actions;
- measure cost and defensive value together at production scale.

The volume also highlights a procurement issue: token economics, model competition and usage controls are becoming part of cyber-resilience planning.
