Europe’s open-weight ecosystem is becoming more multilingual and more deployable.

SpeakLeash has released Bielik-11B-v3.1-Instruct, an 11-billion-parameter model covering 56 languages under the Apache 2.0 licence. GGUF and AWQ versions are also available, making local testing and smaller-footprint deployment easier.

Why this matters for enterprise teams:
- multilingual coverage can improve regional use cases;
- permissive licensing supports controlled experimentation;
- quantised variants widen the range of viable on-premise deployments.

As always, availability is only the starting point. Teams still need task-specific quality, safety, bias, latency and total-cost evaluation before production use.
