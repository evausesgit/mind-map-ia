The important part of agentic commerce is not the shopping demo. It is the control plane.

Walmart’s new MCP-based Agent Gateway will let approved agents search and transact. Agent Profiles will distinguish agent activity from human purchases, with transaction approval, spending limits and revocation.

Those controls map directly to enterprise agent design:
- give every agent a distinct identity;
- grant task-specific permissions and limits;
- log actions and preserve human accountability.

Banks and regulated companies should treat agents as delegated actors — not invisible extensions of a user session.
