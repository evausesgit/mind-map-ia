Public markets are giving AI companies a faster and less forgiving feedback loop.

Anthropic shares fell below their $83 IPO price on 24 November, only days after the Claude developer’s Nasdaq debut. Reuters estimated that the reversal put nearly $200 billion of market value at risk of being erased.

This is more than a short-term price move. Investors are testing whether frontier-model revenue can keep pace with:
- infrastructure and inference costs;
- intense model competition;
- the time needed to convert adoption into durable margins.

For enterprise buyers, vendor resilience should include financial sustainability and capital dependence alongside model performance and security.
