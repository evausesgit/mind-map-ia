AI infrastructure is becoming a credit-market story—not only a technology story.

Elon Musk says SpaceX has closed $40 billion of debt financing for GPUs. The full lender group, covenants and collateral have not yet been disclosed publicly, so the structure matters as much as the headline amount.

For banks and AI leaders, three questions stand out:
- What cash flows support the debt if compute prices fall?
- How is rapid GPU obsolescence reflected in collateral and covenants?
- How concentrated is the exposure to one hardware supplier and one energy-intensive build-out?

The next phase of AI scaling will be shaped by financing discipline, not just model capability.
