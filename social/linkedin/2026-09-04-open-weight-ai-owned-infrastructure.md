Open-weight AI is increasingly being framed as infrastructure that organizations can own, adapt, and govern—not just model access rented through an API.

That distinction matters. Open weights can support private deployment, stronger data-residency controls, deeper auditability, and resilience against supplier concentration. They also shift more operational responsibility to the enterprise.

For regulated sectors, the decision is not open versus closed. It is which workloads require:
- maximum frontier capability;
- local control and inspection;
- predictable cost and exit options.

Open-weight models are becoming part of serious enterprise architecture discussions.
