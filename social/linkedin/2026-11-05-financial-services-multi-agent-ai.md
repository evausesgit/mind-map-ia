Financial services is moving from individual agents to multi-agent systems — but operating maturity is still scarce.

Capgemini’s survey of 1,100 financial-services executives across 14 markets finds that 59% of institutions are piloting or deploying multi-agent AI, while only 10% have enterprise-wide orchestration. Just 5% have institutionalized the capabilities required to operate agentic AI at scale, and 90% use or plan intelligent model routing to balance performance and token cost.

The strategic question is no longer whether to experiment with agents. It is whether the operating model can control identity, data access, model routing, cost, observability and human accountability across interacting agents.

- Start with measurable business outcomes, not agent count.
- Make routing, cost and auditability platform capabilities.
- Scale autonomy only as governance evidence improves.
