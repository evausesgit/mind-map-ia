“Access to the weights” no longer tells us whether a model is truly open.

Microsoft's Licensed Weights approach lets enterprises customize models with proprietary data while keeping use subject to contractual restrictions. It creates a middle ground between closed APIs and fully open releases.

That can be useful, but governance teams need to ask precise questions:

- What fine-tuning, redistribution and deployment rights are granted?
- Can the model artefacts and training lineage be audited?
- Which controls remain dependent on Microsoft?

For regulated firms, licence terms are part of model risk. Technical portability without legal and operational portability is not full independence.
