AI governance needs measurable triggers, not only broad principles.

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki has proposed using model perplexity as a durable, industry-wide measure of general AI progress. The idea is to agree on capability thresholds and avoid crossing them until monitoring and understanding have reached defined milestones.

For enterprise AI leaders, this is relevant beyond frontier labs:
- risk limits need observable metrics and escalation thresholds;
- model upgrades should trigger renewed controls and testing;
- voluntary frameworks still need independent assurance and enforceability.

This is a proposal, not a binding standard. Its value is in shifting the debate from “move fast or slow down” toward what can actually be measured, governed and audited.
