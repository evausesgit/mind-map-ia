The AI investment debate is shifting from adoption to economics.

At an investor event, Anthropic presented figures indicating $16.8B in revenue for the three months to September, up 45% from the prior quarter, and said it was profitable on an adjusted basis. The BBC notes that these are company figures, not public audited accounts.

For markets and enterprise AI leaders, three issues stand out:
- whether revenue growth can keep pace with infrastructure spending;
- how much demand is durable enterprise usage rather than experimentation;
- whether capital-market pressure changes product, pricing, and safety decisions.

A potential listing would give investors more visibility into frontier-model economics. Until then, valuation narratives should be separated from audited operating evidence.
