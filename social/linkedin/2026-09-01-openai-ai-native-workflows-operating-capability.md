The enterprise AI conversation is shifting from model access to workflow ownership.

OpenAI published guidance on how AI-native companies turn workflows into operating capability. The emphasis is on redesigning processes, controls and adoption loops rather than simply adding chat interfaces to existing tools.

For AI leaders, the takeaway is straightforward:
- value measurement should attach to business workflows;
- governance must be embedded where work is actually executed;
- adoption depends on process redesign, not just model quality.

This is especially relevant in regulated environments, where operating capability includes evidence, approvals, human oversight and resilience.
