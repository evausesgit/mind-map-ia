AI agents are starting to move beyond screens and APIs.

Anthropic’s research preview of a Model Hardware Standard is an early signal of what happens when AI systems need to interact with scientific instruments, robotics and other physical tools.

For builders and enterprise AI teams, the interesting point is not just capability. It is control:
- who is allowed to trigger an action;
- how the action is logged and audited;
- what safety boundary prevents an autonomous system from doing the wrong thing.

This is a useful reminder that “agent governance” will increasingly mean infrastructure governance, not only prompt review or model evaluation.
