Open-weight models keep improving the strategic option value of self-hosted AI.

DeepSeek has released V4.1-Flash-1017 under an MIT licence, claiming gains across reasoning, coding, agentic and vision tasks with shorter outputs and faster completion. The weights are available on Hugging Face.

For enterprises, an open licence is only the start. The real evaluation must include:
- independent workload benchmarks and multilingual quality;
- infrastructure and inference cost;
- provenance, security testing and operational controls.

Open weights can improve portability and negotiating leverage, but they do not create compliance by themselves.
