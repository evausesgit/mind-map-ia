Independent testing is moving from a research request to a central frontier-AI policy question.

OpenAI and Anthropic now argue that the most capable models should be independently evaluated before release. That is directionally important, but independence depends on the operating model: who chooses the evaluator, what access they receive, what findings become public, and who can delay a deployment?

For regulated enterprises, the same questions apply internally:
- separate model ownership from validation authority;
- test tool use and real permissions, not only benchmark responses;
- preserve evidence for risk committees and regulators.

External evaluation can strengthen trust, provided it is transparent enough to be more than a vendor-controlled assurance process.
