AI infrastructure is becoming more specialized around enterprise constraints, not just raw speed.

Google introduced TPU 8tx for confidential inference and TPU 8i Max for memory-intensive, million-token workloads. The former protects sensitive data and model weights while in use; the latter doubles compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth by combining two TPU 8i chips.

For regulated enterprises, this shifts infrastructure evaluation toward:
- protection of data in use;
- memory and context economics;
- portability across hardware and software stacks.

The strategic question is no longer simply GPU versus TPU. It is which workload belongs on which accelerator, under what security model, and with what exit plan.
