The AI infrastructure boom is increasingly a financing story.

CoreWeave reported $3.66 billion in quarterly revenue, up 168% year over year, but also a $709 million net loss and $846 million in interest expense. It expects roughly $9 billion in customer prepayments and deposits over three months while financing a $132.5 billion backlog.

The operating trade-off is clear:
- prepayments reduce immediate borrowing needs;
- shorter contracts capture higher prices;
- both depend on durable demand and customer concentration.

For enterprise buyers, capacity contracts are becoming strategic commitments with counterparty, pricing, portability, and concentration implications—not simply cloud procurement.
