Open-weight AI is forcing a harder conversation about capability and control.

Moonshot AI has introduced Kimi K3.1 Pro with restrictions intended to reduce offensive hacking capability, while indicating that model weights are planned for release by November.

That combination matters:
- safeguards at the hosted-product layer may not survive weight release;
- downstream deployers inherit more responsibility for access and monitoring;
- cyber evaluations need to test the deployable artefact, not only the public chatbot.

For banks, “open” should trigger a precise governance discussion covering provenance, licence, model modifications, isolation and misuse monitoring—not an automatic yes or no.
