The AI investment cycle is now being tested by the cost of capital—not only by technical progress.

US equities extended their decline on November 20, with Reuters reporting that the S&P 500 and Dow had entered correction territory as investors questioned AI valuations and the returns on infrastructure spending.

For enterprise AI leaders, the signal is not to stop investing. It is to make the investment case more measurable:
- connect use cases to realized productivity or revenue;
- stress-test vendor and infrastructure concentration;
- separate strategic capability from valuation momentum.

A market correction does not invalidate enterprise demand. It does raise the standard of evidence for every large AI commitment.
