A small open-weight model can enter an institutional stack almost invisibly. The US Federal Register removed two search modes built on Alibaba’s Qwen3-0.6B after their use attracted public attention.

Qwen3-0.6B is distributed under Apache 2.0, which makes adoption technically easy. That convenience also exposes a governance gap:

- Maintain a complete inventory of models and embeddings.
- Record origin, licence, hosting location and approved use.
- Require explicit review for third-country and supply-chain risk.

This is less a story about one model than about component provenance. In complex AI systems, governance has to reach every model in the retrieval and inference chain—not only the model visible to users.
