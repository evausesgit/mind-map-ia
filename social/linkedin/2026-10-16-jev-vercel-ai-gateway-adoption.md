The next phase of enterprise AI may be driven as much by small decision models as by frontier LLMs.

Vercel says Jev, from TypeSafe AI, reached roughly 35% of paid AI Gateway teams in its first fourteen days—the fastest early adoption measured on the platform. The metric is platform-specific, not a general benchmark, but it points to strong demand for fast, inexpensive models that route, classify and constrain outputs.

That matters because many production workflows do not need a large generative model at every step. A layered architecture can use specialised decision models for predictable tasks and reserve expensive frontier models for genuinely complex reasoning.

The practical question is not “Which single model should we standardise on?” It is “How do we govern a portfolio of models without multiplying operational risk?”
