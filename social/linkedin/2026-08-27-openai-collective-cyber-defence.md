AI security is moving from a model-risk topic to a collective-resilience topic.

OpenAI has published a call for action on cyber defence backed by more than 100 organisations. The core message is practical: as AI lowers the cost of reconnaissance, phishing, vulnerability discovery and attack scaling, institutions need stronger identity controls, software-supply-chain security and faster incident sharing.

For regulated firms, this is not just a CISO issue. It affects AI adoption patterns, third-party risk, agent permissions and board-level accountability for operational resilience.

Key takeaways:
- AI-enabled cyber risk is expected to become more sophisticated within months.
- Defence needs to cover identity, infrastructure, software supply chains and information sharing.
- Financial institutions should connect AI governance and cyber governance more tightly.
