For financial institutions, the open-model question is shifting from “is the model good enough?” to “where can it run safely?”

Hugging Face and Farsight describe an on-premises pattern for using open models on pitch books, memos and financial models, backed by managed SSO, network restrictions, approved-resource policies and audit logs.

It is a vendor perspective, not an independent benchmark. Still, the operating-model questions are the right ones:
- Can sensitive data remain inside the institution’s control boundary?
- Can access, model approval and revocation integrate with existing governance?
- Is portability reducing dependency, or simply creating a new stack to maintain?

For AI leaders in banking, the deployment location is becoming part of model-risk management, not merely an infrastructure choice.
