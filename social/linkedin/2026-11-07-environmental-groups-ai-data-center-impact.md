AI's value and its footprint have to be managed together.

Reuters interviewed 15 environmental, conservation and development organisations. Every one uses AI—for climate analysis, wildfire tracking or wildlife monitoring—and every one is concerned about the electricity, water and emissions associated with data centers.

For enterprise AI leaders, the lesson is practical:
- measure workload-level energy and infrastructure exposure;
- include sustainability in architecture and vendor decisions;
- prioritise use cases where measurable value justifies the footprint.

This is not an argument to stop deploying AI. It is an argument for treating resource efficiency as an engineering and governance requirement.
