UK Google Cloud outage disrupts digital banks and public services — and the practical signal matters more than the headline.

Google said its UK cloud services recovered after an hours-long outage disrupted financial firms and public-sector websites. The incident reinforces the need for tested failover, concentration-risk controls and explicit recovery objectives for cloud-hosted AI and banking workloads.

For enterprise AI leaders, three questions follow:
- What dependency or concentration risk does this expose?
- Which controls need to be tested before production deployment?
- How will we measure business value, resilience and total cost over time?

The strategic advantage will come from disciplined implementation, not announcement volume.
