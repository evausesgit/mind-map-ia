Prompts can become evidence. That changes how enterprises should treat them.

A US federal judge has ruled that an expert witness in a climate-related lawsuit involving a Shell subsidiary must disclose details of her AI use, including prompts. The decision is case-specific, but it illustrates a broader direction: when AI contributes to expert or decision-grade work, reproducibility and provenance can become legal requirements.

Enterprise teams should plan for:
- prompt and model-version retention;
- traceable source material and human review;
- clear rules for privilege, confidentiality and litigation holds.

Prompt governance is no longer just an engineering concern.
