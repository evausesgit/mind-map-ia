Agentic AI is not creating entirely new attack techniques. It is making familiar ones cheaper, faster and easier to scale.

TrendAI researchers found exposed infrastructure showing CyberStrikeAI coordinating 44 specialised agents across reconnaissance, vulnerability validation, exploitation, persistence, exfiltration and reporting. At least 19 identifiable organisations, including 16 in Japan, were subjected to validation activity.

The practical lessons are direct:
- reduce external exposure and eliminate default credentials;
- shorten the path from vulnerability disclosure to remediation;
- monitor machine-speed reconnaissance and repeated validation patterns.

For AI and security leaders, agentic offensive tooling raises the baseline. Identity controls, asset inventories, egress monitoring and patch discipline need to operate at a speed closer to the systems now probing them.
