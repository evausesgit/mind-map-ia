The AI trade bounced—but the volatility is the real signal.

After a sharp two-day sell-off, the Nasdaq rose 1.1%, Nvidia gained 2.8% and CoreWeave 6.3%. Yet the S&P 500 still recorded its worst week since April 2025 as investors reassessed AI spending, financing risk and the regulatory implications of the US midterms.

For AI leaders, market repricing can quickly affect:
- infrastructure funding and supplier health;
- pressure to prove return on AI capex;
- board-level scrutiny of vendor concentration.

The strategic question is no longer whether to invest in AI, but how to preserve optionality when capital costs and policy expectations change.
