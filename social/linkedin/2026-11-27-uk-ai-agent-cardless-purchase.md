Nine mini pizzas are a small order—but the payment architecture behind them is strategically important.

In an FCA sandbox trial involving Lloyds Bank, TrueLayer and Papa John’s, an AI agent completed an account-to-account purchase without receiving the customer’s bank credentials. The mandate included explicit permission and transaction and monthly spending limits.

Three questions now move to the foreground:
- Who is liable when the agent chooses incorrectly?
- How are consent, limits and revocation represented?
- What evidence is retained for audit and disputes?

For banks and merchants, agentic commerce will depend less on conversational polish than on trustworthy identity, authorization and transaction controls.
