The most important AI-cyber shift may be speed, not novelty.

A Wall Street Journal review describes AI-assisted attacks arriving faster and at greater volume, shortening the window defenders have to identify exposure, patch systems and contain incidents.

For regulated enterprises, this changes the operating model:
- identity and privilege controls must assume automated probing;
- telemetry needs to support rapid, cross-system correlation;
- incident response should be rehearsed for machine-speed escalation.

AI security is no longer a specialist model-risk topic. It is becoming part of core cyber resilience and operational risk.
