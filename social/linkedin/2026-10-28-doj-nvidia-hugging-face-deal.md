The proposed Nvidia–Hugging Face deal is not only an M&A story. It is an AI infrastructure and distribution story.

For enterprise AI, the concentration questions are concrete:
- who controls model discovery and distribution;
- whether hardware, hosting and developer tooling become more tightly bundled;
- how open ecosystems preserve portability and neutral access.

AI leaders should track the deal through the lens of vendor dependency, exit options and model-supply resilience.
