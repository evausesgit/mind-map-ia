AI infrastructure risk is not only about chips, power, or supply chains. It is also about governance.

Nvidia’s board was reportedly meeting to decide Jensen Huang’s future. The story was still developing at publication time, so the outcome should not be assumed.

For enterprise AI leaders, three questions matter:
- How exposed is the roadmap to one executive or one supplier?
- What continuity plans exist for critical accelerator capacity and software?
- Are governance events included in vendor-risk monitoring?

Nvidia sits at the centre of the global AI compute stack. Any leadership disruption therefore has consequences well beyond one listed company.
