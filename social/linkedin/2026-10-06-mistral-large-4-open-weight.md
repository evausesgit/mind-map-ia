Europe now has a new frontier-scale open-weight model to evaluate—not just another API to consume.

Mistral has launched an API preview of Mistral Large 4, a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 1 trillion total parameters and 49 billion active parameters. The company says it trained ML4 on 3,800 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs in European data centres and will release the weights by the end of October.

For enterprise AI leaders, three aspects deserve attention:
- self-deployment and European infrastructure can reduce some sovereignty and concentration risks;
- cybersecurity, finance and agentic workflows are explicit target domains;
- vendor benchmarks remain claims to validate on internal workloads, controls and total cost.

The real test will begin when the weights, licence details and reproducible evaluations are available.
