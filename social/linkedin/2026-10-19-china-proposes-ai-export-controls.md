Model weights are becoming strategic trade goods.

China has proposed export controls covering 23 AI-related technologies, including large models, agents, chip designs and training clusters. Public reporting indicates that open-source tools and public research would generally remain exempt, but the final scope and implementation still matter.

Enterprise teams should map exposure now:
- Which deployed components originate in China?
- Are weights mirrored and legally portable?
- Could licensing, updates or hosted access change after a control decision?

The lesson is to treat model provenance, licence terms and jurisdictional dependencies as first-class architecture data.
