OpenAI is moving from general-purpose enterprise AI toward deeply configured professional workflows. Astra for Law combines GPT-6 Astra with legal instructions and a search index spanning more than 230 million URLs.

Initial access is aimed at selected Am Law 200 firms and API partners. Notable control commitments include zero data retention for the API and no OpenAI human review of ChatGPT Enterprise content.

The practical lesson extends beyond legal work:

- Domain grounding is becoming part of the product, not an add-on.
- Data-handling terms can be as important as model quality.
- Buyers still need independent validation for high-consequence outputs.

For regulated firms, the competitive question is shifting from “Which model?” to “Which domain stack, controls and evidence?”
