Frontier-model release cadence is becoming a governance decision, not just a product decision.

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has called on leading AI companies to slow model development as concerns grow around misuse and loss of control. The important point is less the headline than the coordination problem: one lab slowing alone may simply transfer competitive advantage to another.

For regulated enterprises, this strengthens the case for:
- capability-based deployment gates rather than vendor release dates;
- independent red-team evidence and rollback plans;
- contractual incident disclosure and model-change notification.

AI leaders should assume that model velocity and safety expectations will remain in tension—and design procurement and governance accordingly.
