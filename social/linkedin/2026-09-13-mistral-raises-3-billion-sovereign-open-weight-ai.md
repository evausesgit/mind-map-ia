Sovereign AI is becoming a capital-intensive infrastructure strategy, not only a policy slogan.

Mistral has raised a €3 billion Series D led by Samsung Electronics at a post-money valuation above €21 billion. BNP Paribas CIB is among the existing investors participating in the round. Mistral says it will use the funding to expand frontier research, compute capacity, commercial growth and its international footprint.

The strategic proposition combines:
- open-weight models that customers can control and customise;
- private, predictable compute and deployment choices;
- auditability and reduced dependence on a single vendor’s roadmap.

Mistral reports operations in 20 countries and more than 125 enterprise customers. For European banks, the important question is now execution: whether open-weight and sovereign stacks can deliver competitive capability while meeting resilience, security, data-location and total-cost requirements at scale.
