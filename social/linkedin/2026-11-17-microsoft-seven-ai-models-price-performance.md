Microsoft is making model economics a first-class product feature — not an afterthought.

At Ignite, the company introduced seven in-house AI models across coding, cyber defence, image editing, realtime voice, transcription and decision tasks. Its headline claims are striking: MAI Cyber 2 Flash allegedly exceeds GPT-6 Astra on CyberGymE2E at under 2% of the cost per task, while MAI Code 2 reaches 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol coding quality at roughly 40% of the price.

Three takeaways for enterprise AI teams:
- route workloads by task instead of defaulting to one frontier model;
- validate vendor benchmarks on internal data and risk scenarios;
- measure cost per completed task, including retries and human review, not only token price.

The strategic shift is clear: sustainable agent deployment will depend as much on unit economics and operational controls as on headline capability.
