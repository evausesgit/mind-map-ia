Open-weight models make alignment choices inspectable — and contestable.

Nous Research has released Arete-27B, an Apache-2.0 model post-trained from Qwen3.8-27B using supervised fine-tuning. The release includes training data and an 803-prompt evaluation focused on factual recall and political framing.

Nous reports a fact-score increase from 2.32 to 2.98 out of 5 and a measured propaganda-rate reduction from 22% to 0.9%. These are developer-reported results, and the model card appropriately notes that some comparison models used different decoding settings.

The broader lesson for regulated enterprises: value alignment is part of model risk. Evaluation needs documented rubrics, reproducible harnesses, multilingual testing and independent challenge — not only aggregate capability scores.
