Anthropic says Claude now leads roughly one quarter of the work involved in building its next generation of models. That is a meaningful shift from AI as a coding assistant to AI as part of the model-development loop itself.

The productivity upside is clear. So is the governance challenge:

- AI-generated research and engineering decisions need traceable review.
- Evaluation must remain independent from the system helping to build the model.
- Human accountability cannot be delegated with the task.

For enterprise AI leaders, this is a preview of a broader operating-model change: controls need to cover not only what models do in production, but how AI contributes to designing, testing and updating the systems we rely on.
