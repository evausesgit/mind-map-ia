AI agents are becoming a production architecture, not just a demo pattern. The challenge is that enterprise operating rules are still catching up with systems that can plan, call tools and change state.

For organizations, this creates a governance gap: who approved the action, what permissions were used, what evidence was retained, and who is accountable if an agent causes an incident?

Practical implications:
- Agent permissions need to be scoped like privileged access.
- Human approval points should be designed into high-impact workflows.
- Logs and audit trails must explain both the instruction and the action taken.

Agent adoption will be limited less by imagination than by trust, controls and incident readiness.
