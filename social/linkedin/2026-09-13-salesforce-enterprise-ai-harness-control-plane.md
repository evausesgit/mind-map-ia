As agents move from pilots into business processes, enterprises need a control architecture around them—not a separate governance project for every bot.

Salesforce has introduced an Enterprise AI Harness spanning six capabilities: context, agency, action, governance, security and model choice. A new AI Control Plane is designed to register agents, apply identity and policy, observe performance and outcomes, manage lifecycle and control costs across Salesforce and third-party systems.

The useful architectural idea is separation of concerns:
- open-ended model reasoning can remain flexible;
- high-impact execution can be constrained by deterministic policies;
- agent identity, permissions and telemetry can be managed consistently.

For regulated firms, the test will be whether this control plane integrates with existing IAM, data lineage, model risk, change management and operational resilience processes. A unified dashboard is valuable only if the underlying evidence is complete, exportable and independently testable.
