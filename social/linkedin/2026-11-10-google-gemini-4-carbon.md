The frontier-model race is shifting from single answers to long-horizon execution.

Google has made Gemini 4 Carbon available to paying developers and enterprises at an introductory $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. It targets complex coding, financial knowledge work, scientific reasoning and cybersecurity, while Google is previewing multi-agent orchestration through /swarm.

The enterprise question is not whether Carbon tops a generic leaderboard. It is whether it improves completion rate, verification quality and cost per successful task on governed internal workflows.

That requires side-by-side tests with fixed data, tool permissions, retry budgets and human-review thresholds.
