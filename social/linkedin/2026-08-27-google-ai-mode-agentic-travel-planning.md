Google’s AI Mode is moving from answering questions toward helping users complete tasks.

TechCrunch reports that the product can now track flight prices, help book hotels, and support more travel-planning workflows. It is a consumer feature, but the direction is strategically important: search is becoming more agentic.

For enterprise teams, this is a useful signal for internal AI roadmaps. Users will increasingly expect assistants that do not just summarize information, but orchestrate steps across tools.

Implications:
- Workflow design matters as much as model selection.
- Permissioning and audit trails become core UX requirements.
- Agent evaluation should include task completion and failure modes.
