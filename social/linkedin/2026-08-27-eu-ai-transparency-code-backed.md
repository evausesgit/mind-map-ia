AI transparency is becoming an operational control, not a policy slogan.

The European Commission says its Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content has strong backing, as AI Act transparency obligations start to apply.

For regulated firms, the direction is clear:
- synthetic content needs labelling and traceability;
- customer-facing AI needs stronger governance evidence;
- internal controls must cover generated market, research and communication content.

This is where legal, compliance, technology and AI leadership need a shared implementation plan.
