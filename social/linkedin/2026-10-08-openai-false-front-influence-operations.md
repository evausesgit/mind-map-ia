AI-enabled influence operations are moving beyond fake social posts.

OpenAI says it disrupted Russian- and Iranian-origin operations that combined ChatGPT with traditional tactics, including fake journalists, fabricated documents and a purported think tank. Some content reached external media outlets, giving the campaigns greater potential impact than closed social-media loops.

The enterprise lesson is broader than content moderation:
- verify identities and provenance across communications workflows;
- treat synthetic text, audio and documents as an integrated threat surface;
- connect AI governance with cyber, fraud, communications and insider-risk controls.

Detection cannot rely on writing style alone. Process controls and source verification are becoming the stronger defence.
