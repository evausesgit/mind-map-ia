DeepSeek’s V4.1-Flash is a useful reminder that inference architecture—not only benchmark quality—is shaping the economics of enterprise AI.

The open-weight multimodal MoE has 552B total parameters, with 8B active for input and 16B for output. DeepSeek reports that its asymmetric design reduces KV-cache requirements to one quarter of the HBM and one eighth of the SSD storage used by the previous generation. Weights and a technical report are available on Hugging Face.

The practical questions for enterprises are now:
- can the claimed memory and throughput gains be reproduced on target infrastructure?
- does self-hosting improve control enough to offset validation and operational burden?
- how should provenance, licensing and security testing be governed?

Efficiency gains matter most when they survive independent evaluation and production workloads.
