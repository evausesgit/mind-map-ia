Not every enterprise AI problem needs a general-purpose frontier model.

Cloudflare has released open weights for Clairic 27B and Clairic-flash 9B, models designed for typed extraction from text, images and PDFs. They return confidence information and are also available as hosted Workers AI endpoints.

This task-specific approach matters because extraction workloads often need:
- predictable schemas and latency;
- deployment flexibility and data control;
- transparent testing against real documents and exception cases.

Cloudflare reports sub-second latency in its own evaluations. Independent, domain-specific validation should be the next step—especially for regulated documents.
