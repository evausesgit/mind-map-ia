Open models can create value far beyond general-purpose chatbots.

IBM and NASA have released an open-source multimodal foundation model for lunar science, together with a machine-learning-ready dataset covering more than 30 aligned layers from nine instruments and four missions.

Reported results include:

- up to 22% lower error when identifying areas with potential lunar ice;
- nearly 19% better context-scale crater detection than SwinV2-B;
- half the training data for that crater-detection comparison.

The broader lesson for enterprise AI is the importance of domain models built on carefully aligned, governed datasets. Openness also enables independent validation, adaptation, and shared scientific progress.
