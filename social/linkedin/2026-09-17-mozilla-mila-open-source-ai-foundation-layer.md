Open models are widely available. Turning them into a secure, governed production service remains the harder problem.

Mozilla has committed an initial $5 million and Hypertec another $1 million to a Mila-led initiative for an open-standard AI foundation layer. The project plans a swappable stack and reference implementation that organisations can run locally, with governance and access controls included.

Mozilla reports that 79% of developers adding AI use open models, but only 53% of teams reach production. The gap points to the real adoption bottlenecks: integration, security, maintenance and operating cost.

For regulated enterprises, a credible reference stack could improve portability and data control. Its value will depend on hardened defaults, lifecycle support, measurable controls and a genuinely interoperable standard.
