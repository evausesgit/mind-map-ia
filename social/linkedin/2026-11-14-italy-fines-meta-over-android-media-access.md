Data access is not a technical detail. It is a governance decision.

Italy’s data-protection authority fined Meta €805 million ($887 million), saying the company accessed Android users’ photographs and videos without adequate legal grounds.

For AI-enabled products, this reinforces three controls that must exist before model evaluation begins:
- explicit purpose and lawful basis;
- granular permissions and provenance;
- auditable retention, reuse and deletion rules.

The larger the data opportunity, the more important it is to prove that access is authorised — not merely possible.
