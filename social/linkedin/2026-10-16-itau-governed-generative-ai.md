The most useful banking AI case studies increasingly focus less on the chatbot and more on the control plane behind it.

Itaú says its ia.i assistant started with around 300,000 customers, supported by a financial-context layer covering roughly 70 million people. Its IARA platform controls access to multiple models, token cost and responsible-AI policies.

The operating model is the interesting part:
- One governed platform rather than isolated model integrations.
- Explicit cost, accuracy and policy controls.
- Human support remains part of the customer journey.

Itaú also reports more than two million monthly legal tasks across 90 million documents, with 20%–80% efficiency gains. Those figures are not independently audited, but the architecture illustrates the real enterprise challenge: scale AI through observable, policy-aware workflows—not scattered pilots.
