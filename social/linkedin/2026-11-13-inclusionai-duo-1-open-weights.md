Real-time multimodal AI is moving from turn-taking toward simultaneous interaction.

InclusionAI has released Duo 1.0 weights and code for full-duplex audio-visual interaction. Its distilled variant is designed to perceive and generate audio and video concurrently, rather than waiting for one participant to finish before responding.

That creates interesting possibilities for assistants, training and support workflows—but it also raises the bar for evaluation:
- interruption handling and latency become product-quality metrics;
- audio and video permissions need explicit governance;
- teams must review the MiniMax H3 community licence before commercial deployment.

Open weights improve inspectability and experimentation. They do not remove the need for security, licensing and human-factors testing.
