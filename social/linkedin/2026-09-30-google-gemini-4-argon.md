Frontier-model pricing is getting easier to quote—and harder to evaluate.

Google announced Gemini 4 Argon for long-horizon software engineering, enterprise knowledge work and cyber defence. Introductory API pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with a 95% cached-input discount. Access is being phased, beginning with trusted cyber defenders.

For enterprise buyers, the token price is only the first line of the business case:
- agent loops can multiply usage;
- observability and controls add operating cost;
- provider benchmarks need reproduction on real workflows.

The strongest evaluation will combine quality, latency, security, human-review effort and total cost per completed process.
