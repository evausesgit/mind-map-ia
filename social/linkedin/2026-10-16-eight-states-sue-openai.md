Consumer AI safety is becoming litigation risk.

Eight US states have sued OpenAI, according to AP, alleging consumer-protection violations, harm to children and misleading conduct toward adults. These are allegations, not findings, and they will now be tested in court.

For enterprise AI governance, the lesson is broader:
- safety claims need evidence;
- age and vulnerability controls need clear ownership;
- incidents and design decisions need an auditable trail.

Product speed does not reduce accountability. It increases the need to prove how risks were identified and controlled.
