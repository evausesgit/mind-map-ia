AI safety may be moving toward an industry-wide control layer.

Representatives from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google have reportedly met regularly since July to discuss a joint body for model testing and auditing. One model under discussion is FINRA-style self-regulation while governments build formal rules.

Three questions will determine whether this is credible:
- Can the body audit members independently?
- Will standards be enforceable rather than voluntary?
- Will incidents, test methods and exceptions be disclosed?

For regulated enterprises, shared standards could simplify model due diligence and third-party risk management. But they should complement—not replace—independent supervision, internal controls and institution-specific validation.
