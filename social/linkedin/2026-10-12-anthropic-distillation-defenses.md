Model distillation is becoming a first-class AI security issue.

Anthropic says three new defenses reduced data harvested from Claude through illicit distillation attempts by roughly 90% to 99%. The result is vendor-reported, but the direction is important: API abuse detection is moving beyond rate limits toward behavioural signals and protection of reasoning traces.

Enterprise AI teams should translate this into controls of their own:
- monitor automated and distributed extraction patterns;
- separate legitimate model optimisation from prohibited copying;
- include model and prompt intellectual property in threat models.

As agents scale API activity, security teams will need better provenance, anomaly detection and contractual clarity — not just stronger authentication.
