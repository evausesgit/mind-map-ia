The business model for consumer AI agents is becoming clearer: subscriptions, devices and a fee on transactions completed on the user’s behalf.

At Connect 2026, Meta expanded Muse into new wearables, including the Muse Charm expected by the holidays. Mark Zuckerberg also said Meta would take a small fee from Muse transactions.

That shift turns agent governance into a commercial-control question:
- who owns the customer relationship;
- which merchants and services an agent may access;
- how consent, authentication and disputed actions are handled.

For financial institutions, agentic commerce will require stronger delegated-authority rules, transaction-level auditability and clear liability across model, platform, merchant and payment provider.
