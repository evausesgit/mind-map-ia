Anthropic is testing a more granular alternative to one-size-fits-all model restrictions. Its new Life Sciences Verification Program gives approved teams more permissive biology safeguards across Mythos, Opus and Sonnet.

The beta supports work including drug discovery, research biology, clinical development and manufacturing. Dozens of organizations joined early access, but BAA-enabled organizations handling protected health information are not yet supported.

This is a useful governance pattern for high-risk domains:

- Verify the institution and intended use.
- Grant capability according to risk tier.
- Preserve monitoring and revocation mechanisms.

For regulated enterprises, differentiated access may offer a better balance between innovation and control than blanket enablement or blanket prohibition.
