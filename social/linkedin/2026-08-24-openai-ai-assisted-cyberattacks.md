AI-assisted cyber risk is becoming an operating reality, not a future scenario.

OpenAI has warned that AI-enabled attacks are becoming more persistent. The concern is clear: models and agents can reduce the cost of reconnaissance, phishing, exploit scripting, and scaled vulnerability probing.

For regulated enterprises, the response cannot be limited to awareness training. It requires engineering controls around agent access, tool permissions, logging, and incident response.

A useful baseline:
- Treat AI agents as identities, not just applications.
- Scope what they can read, write, and trigger.
- Make human approval explicit for sensitive workflows.

The productivity upside is real — but so is the need for auditable control.
