“Self-driving company” is a provocative phrase. The useful part is not the slogan—it is the operating-model question behind it.

Replit says its agents now investigate support issues and operational work while employees focus more on architecture and higher-level decisions. The company reports that engineering code output nearly tripled over six months without worse review times, reversions or incidents, though these results are self-reported.

For regulated enterprises, autonomy should be decomposed into explicit controls:
- what the agent may observe;
- what it may decide;
- what it may execute;
- where human approval remains mandatory.

The destination is not a company without people. It is an organisation where routine execution is automated without making accountability ambiguous.
