European AI infrastructure is becoming a practical procurement choice, not only a policy objective.

OVHcloud has launched 55 cloud and AI products hosted on European infrastructure, including access to Nvidia's latest AI chips and a workplace application.

For regulated enterprises, the real evaluation starts after the word “European”:

- where data, logs and backups actually reside;
- which subcontractors and support teams can access them;
- whether capacity, SLAs, reversibility and total cost meet production needs.

More credible options can improve resilience and negotiating leverage. Sovereignty still has to be demonstrated through architecture, contracts and operational controls.
