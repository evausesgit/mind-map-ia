California is moving from AI safety disclosure toward independent verification.

Governor Gavin Newsom has ordered faster implementation of the state’s new oversight laws and asked experts to propose stronger safeguards within two months. Options include onsite independent assessors, validation of safety reports and an emergency shutoff for frontier models whose effectiveness would be tested continuously.

Three governance signals stand out:
- self-attestation is increasingly unlikely to satisfy regulators;
- audit independence and technical access will matter;
- shutdown and containment mechanisms may need demonstrable evidence, not policy language alone.

Enterprises should expect similar expectations to flow into vendor due diligence and critical-model exit planning.
