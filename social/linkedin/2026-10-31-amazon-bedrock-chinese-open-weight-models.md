Open-weight models are becoming easier to consume through enterprise cloud controls.

Amazon is expanding Bedrock access to Chinese open-weight model families including Kimi and GLM. AWS describes GLM 5.3 as a mixture-of-experts model with 753 billion total parameters and roughly 40 billion active parameters, aimed at agentic coding and long-horizon software work.

For enterprise teams, managed access changes the adoption equation:
- evaluation can fit existing identity, logging and security patterns;
- model portability and licence terms still require explicit review;
- provenance, jurisdiction and supply-chain risk remain distinct from hosting controls.

The useful question is no longer simply open versus closed. It is which combination of weights, runtime, governance evidence and operational control fits each workload.
