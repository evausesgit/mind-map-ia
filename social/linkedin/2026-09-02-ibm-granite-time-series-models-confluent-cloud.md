Open and specialised AI models are becoming more useful when they move closer to governed enterprise data flows.

IBM and Confluent describe native inference for IBM Granite Time Series models inside Apache Flink on Confluent Cloud, initially on AWS. The target use cases are practical: forecasting and anomaly detection on streaming data.

The signal for builders is important:
- specialised models may be easier to operationalise than general chat models;
- streaming inference reduces latency and data movement;
- governance becomes part of the data pipeline rather than an afterthought.

For financial services, this pattern is relevant to market data quality, operational monitoring, fraud signals and real-time risk controls.
