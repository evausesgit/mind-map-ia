Cybersecurity is becoming a frontier-model battleground, not just another benchmark category.

Google has introduced Gemini 4 Carbon, claiming meaningful gains in coding, agentic tasks and cybersecurity. The important enterprise question is not whether one benchmark moves up, but whether those gains survive realistic controls, restricted tool access and production workloads.

For AI leaders, the evaluation agenda is clear:
- test defensive value and dual-use risk together;
- measure latency and cost on real security workflows;
- keep permissions, audit trails and human escalation outside the model.

A stronger cyber model can accelerate triage and remediation. It can also increase the blast radius of weak governance. Capability and control have to advance together.
