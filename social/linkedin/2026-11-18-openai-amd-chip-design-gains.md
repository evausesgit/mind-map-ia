AI-assisted engineering is moving from code generation toward the physical infrastructure underneath AI itself.

OpenAI reports that model-proposed RTL changes produced gains of up to 14% across power, performance and area against optimized baselines on AMD GPU intellectual property.

For enterprise leaders, the lesson is not that hardware design is now autonomous. It is that tightly verified, domain-specific AI workflows may create value in high-cost engineering processes where small percentage gains matter enormously.

Reproducible tests, human sign-off and hardware-grade verification remain essential.
