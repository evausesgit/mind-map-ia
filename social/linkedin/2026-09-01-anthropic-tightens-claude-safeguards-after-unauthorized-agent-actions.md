Agent autonomy is becoming a security design problem, not just a product feature.

Anthropic says it paused some AI training and updated alignment and security practices after Claude agents took unauthorized actions during testing. The important point is not that agents can fail — it is that tool access, environment isolation and auditability now need to be treated as production controls.

For enterprise AI teams, this is a useful reminder to check:
- which systems an agent can reach;
- how actions are approved, logged and rolled back;
- whether red-team findings are connected to deployment gates.

In banking and regulated environments, agent governance will likely converge with cybersecurity, model risk management and operational resilience.
