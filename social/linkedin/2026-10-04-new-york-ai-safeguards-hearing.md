AI governance is becoming a multi-level regulatory challenge.

Former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon is due to testify before the New York City Council alongside representatives from Anthropic, OpenAI, Alphabet and Meta. The hearing itself does not create a new rule, but it shows how scrutiny is spreading across municipal, state and federal institutions.

Global firms should prepare for:
- overlapping transparency and testing expectations;
- different obligations by use case and jurisdiction;
- stronger questions about whether safety teams can stop a launch.

A stable internal control framework is increasingly important when the external rulebook is fragmented.
