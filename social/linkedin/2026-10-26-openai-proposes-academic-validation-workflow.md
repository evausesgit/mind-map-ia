AI-generated research needs a validation operating model, not just impressive outputs.

OpenAI has presented three claimed results across mathematics, computer science and physics, alongside a proposed workflow for working with external academic experts. The important part is not only what the model produced, but how claims are surfaced, challenged and independently checked.

For enterprise AI, the parallel is clear:
- separate generation from approval;
- preserve evidence and provenance;
- use domain experts who can falsify, not merely endorse, the output.

A strong model does not remove review. It changes where review effort should be concentrated and what evidence must be retained.
