Browser agents are crossing an important boundary: from reading context to taking action.

OpenAI’s updated Chrome extension lets ChatGPT inspect designated tabs. Through ChatGPT Work, it can also perform tasks such as filling forms, sending messages, downloading files, and organising tabs.

For enterprises, the productivity opportunity comes with a new control surface:
- scope access to the minimum required tabs and data;
- require confirmation for consequential actions;
- log browser actions in a form that security and compliance teams can review.

The adoption question is no longer only “Does the model answer well?” It is increasingly “Can the organisation govern what the agent can see and do?”
