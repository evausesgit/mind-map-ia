AI-enabled cyberattacks are becoming a policy and resilience issue, not a future scenario.

The US administration’s AI task force met to discuss the use of AI in cyberattacks. The practical concern is that automation can accelerate reconnaissance, social engineering and attack iteration while compressing defenders’ response time.

Enterprise AI leaders should respond with joint ownership across AI, cyber and operational resilience:
- threat-model agentic and tool-using systems;
- tighten identity, privilege and tool-access controls;
- rehearse incidents in which attackers also use AI.

The key shift is speed: existing attack patterns may become cheaper, more scalable and harder to distinguish from legitimate automated activity.
