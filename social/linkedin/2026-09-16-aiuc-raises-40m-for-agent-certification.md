Enterprise agents need more than trust in a model provider. They need evidence that controls work.

Artificial Intelligence Underwriting Company has raised a $40 million Series A led by Ribbit Capital to expand AIUC-1, a testing and certification framework for AI agents, alongside insurance infrastructure.

The emerging assurance layer is important because agents combine models, tools, permissions and data:
- each integration expands the control surface;
- audit evidence can support procurement and operational-risk decisions;
- insurance can price residual risk, but cannot replace sound controls.

The useful question for enterprises is not whether an agent is “safe” in the abstract, but whether its permissions, failure modes and monitoring are measurable in the deployed workflow.
