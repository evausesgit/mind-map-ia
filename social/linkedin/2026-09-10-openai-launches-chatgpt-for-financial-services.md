The next phase of enterprise AI competition is increasingly vertical—and finance is now a primary battleground.

OpenAI has launched ChatGPT for Financial Services, a finance-specific ChatGPT Work offering designed with Morgan Stanley and Evercore. It combines GPT-6 Astra with hosted financial data from providers including Daloopa, PitchBook, LSEG News, and Crunchbase.

The enterprise controls are as important as the model:
- role-based read and write permissions for connected applications;
- configurable retention and audit-log exports;
- encryption and no customer-data training by default.

For financial institutions, this raises a practical build-versus-buy question. A packaged vertical assistant may accelerate research and document workflows, but firms still need source entitlements, reproducibility, model-risk testing, information barriers, and human accountability for outputs.
