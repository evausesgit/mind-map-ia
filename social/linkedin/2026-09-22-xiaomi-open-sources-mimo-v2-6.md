Open-weight multimodal models are moving closer to the frontier — but the operational footprint remains substantial.

Xiaomi has released MiMo-V2.6 Pro and Flash under the MIT licence. Both support text, image, video and audio with a 1-million-token context window. The Pro checkpoint is a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 1.02 trillion total parameters and 42 billion active; Flash uses 309 billion total and 15 billion active.

Three details stand out:
- Xiaomi trained one mixed reinforcement-learning run across coding, general agents, visual tasks and cybersecurity.
- It also released a 9B Qwen-based distillation checkpoint as a more accessible starting point for agentic research.
- The published benchmark results are vendor-reported and still need independent replication.

For enterprises, open weights create genuine options for control, customisation and deployment — but licence review is only the first step. Security evaluation, infrastructure sizing, data governance and reproducible task-specific benchmarks remain essential.
