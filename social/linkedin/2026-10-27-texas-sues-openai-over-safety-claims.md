AI safety claims are becoming a consumer-protection issue, not only a technical debate.

The Texas attorney general has sued OpenAI, alleging that the company misled consumers about safety and seeking fines and wider remedies. The claims have not been adjudicated, and OpenAI will have an opportunity to respond.

The governance lesson for enterprise teams is broader:
- treat safety statements as evidence-backed product claims;
- align marketing, model cards and actual controls;
- retain testing records that can withstand external scrutiny.

In regulated environments, a gap between documented controls and real-world behaviour can become litigation, conduct and reputational risk at the same time.
