Frontier capability is increasingly being packaged around cost per completed task, not benchmark scores alone.

Google has launched Gemini 4 Neon with a one-million-token context window, API and enterprise availability, and list pricing of $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. The model is positioned for coding, analysis, cybersecurity and long-running agentic workflows.

The practical enterprise questions remain:
- Does it outperform alternatives on our own workflows?
- How stable is tool use over long runs?
- Can we evidence controls for data, prompts, actions and model versions?

The next phase of model competition will be won in production evaluation—not launch-day leaderboards.
